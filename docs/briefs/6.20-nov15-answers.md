## 1. The watermark on a plan sits inside the plan box (Kevin, Nov 15)

Today a plan's PDF and PNG take the storyboard's watermark rule against the whole page, so the stamp crosses the
plan box's frame line and the legend's last rows. Kevin: "its position is taken inside the plan box (that corner,
or the centre, inset from the frame line), so it never covers the legend or the title."

- The project's watermark setting keeps its meaning (which corner, or the centre; its text, size and opacity as
  now): on a plan page the position is resolved against the plan area's box (the boxed drawing area of
  src/features/export/planPages.ts), inset from the frame line by the same margin the storyboard's rule uses from a
  page edge (no smaller than 4 mm on paper), never against the page.
- The whole stamp lies inside the plan box: never over the legend column or band, the header or title, or the frame
  line itself. If the stamp is wider or taller than the box less its insets, scale it down to fit (say so in a
  comment); it is never cut.
- It may sit over the drawing, and over the scale bar only if its corner is the scale bar's (bottom left): then it
  sits above the scale bar, not on it.
- Legend continuation pages carry no stamp (no plan box). Every plan page of a packet and of a department print
  carries it.
- PDF and PNG agree (one function gives the stamp's box; both use it).
- Tests (Vitest): for each position setting (every corner and centre) on A4 and Letter, with the legend at the
  right and as a bottom band, the stamp's box is inside the plan box less the inset and meets neither the legend
  box nor the header; an oversized stamp is scaled, not cut; the bottom-left case clears the scale bar. The
  existing e2e test planExport.spec.ts "a plan's PDF and PNG carry the project's watermark…" compares a corner
  region of the PNG (0.7, 0.9, 0.3 × 0.1 of the page) and the top-left quarter: if the stamp's new place falls
  outside that corner region, this brief allows changing ONLY that region's rectangle to the stamp's new box
  (keep `toBeGreaterThan(100)` and the far region's `toBe(0)`), and say so in the report.

## 2. What a department print shows (Kevin, Nov 15)

"Every department's print shows the room, and the cameras with their cones and the cast marks, dimmed, with that
department's own items in full."

- On every department's page: the room (walls, doors, windows, set pieces, underlay as it prints, north) as it
  prints now; the cameras with their cones (and their labels: letter, lens, field of view) and the cast marks (and
  their paths and labels) as context, dimmed; the department's own items in full ink with their labels, beams,
  heights and watts. Other departments' items (other than cameras and cast marks) are left out, as now.
- "Dimmed" is the one dim the Layers tile already uses on screen for a dimmed layer (the canvas core's `dim`),
  carried to print as real transparency (6.10 carries opacity to the PDF and PNG). One constant; don't invent a
  second value.
- The camera department's own print shows cameras in full (they are its items) and cast marks dimmed; the cast
  (blocking) print shows marks in full and cameras dimmed. Walls stop the dimmed cones exactly as the full ones
  (6.18: the print path cuts as the screen does).
- The legend of a department print lists that department's lines only, as now; add nothing for the context.
- Which departments get a page stays as it is (a department with no items of its own gets none: cameras and
  marks as context never make a page).
- Tests (Vitest, in the plan print tests beside the existing per-department ones): a lighting print's ops contain
  the camera's body and cone and a cast mark at the dim opacity, the light at full; the camera print has cameras
  full and marks dimmed; the blocking print the reverse; a department with only context gets no page; the legend
  has no camera or cast line on a lighting print. The existing tests of per-department prints that assert the
  camera is ABSENT from another department's page change to assert it present and dimmed: this brief allows that
  change in those tests only (name each in the report with old and new lines).
- Probe pictures (rasterise page 1 as 6.10's probe did, pdf.js is in the project): a lighting print and a camera
  print, A4, with the watermark on, to test-results/probe620/.
