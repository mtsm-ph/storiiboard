# Brief 4.10: the map on the call sheet

For the builder (.claude/agents/builder.md). Written by the main session, Nov 10, 2026. Read CLAUDE.md first; its
Safety section comes before this brief. You start with no other context: everything you need is here or in the files
named.

## Goal

Each location on the call sheet, on screen and in the PDF, gets a small map image around its place with the pin and
the tile credit (docs/PHASE4_DECISIONS.md, decision 4b; CLAUDE.md, "The map on paper (4.6) … then a map image from
the tile cache with its credit (4.10)").

Builds on 4.9's map (src-tauri/src/tiles.rs, src/app/mapTiles.ts and its queue src/app/tileQueue.ts (at most 2 out,
only tiles in view: the PDF's few tiles go through the same queue), src/features/locations/LocationMap.tsx,
src/core/model/mercator.ts; the tile policy, the own-provider setting and its credit) and 4.6/4.7's PDF
(src/features/callsheets/callSheetPdf.ts, exportCallSheet.ts, issueCallSheet.ts, fingerprint.ts). Read
docs/briefs/4.9-map.md and 4.6-pdf.md, 4.7-issuing.md first.

## Decisions that apply

- **On screen:** the Call sheet tile's location block shows a read-only map (4.9's component, no pin dragging, no
  pan; zoom 16 centred on the place, about 100% × 160 px) with the pin and the credit. Only shown when the location
  has a place. It's a view like the Locations tile's map, so its tiles load under the same rules and land in the cache.
- **In the PDF:** a map image per location with a place: about 60 mm × 40 mm, zoom 16 centred on the place, the pin
  drawn in vector on top, a 1.5 pt ink frame, and the credit under it ("© OpenStreetMap contributors", or the own
  provider's credit as 4.9 sets it).
  - The tiles come from the tile cache through `map_tile` (4.9), which fetches a missing or stale one under the same
    policy (it's the same few tiles the sheet's map shows: at most 6 per location; never other zooms, never ahead).
  - The image is composed from the PNG tiles in pdf-lib (embed each tile and clip to the frame, or compose them on an
    OffscreenCanvas into one PNG and embed that; pick the one that keeps the PDF smaller and say which).
  - Tiles that can't be had (offline and not cached, switched off, refused): the frame is drawn empty with one line
    ("Map not available offline" or the switch's reason words), and the address, coordinates, link and QR still print.
  - The browser build has no tiles: the PDF draws the frame with the grid 4.9 draws, so tests are deterministic.
  - The forecast isn't involved. Exporting waits for the tiles it asks for, with a time limit (10 s), then prints what
    it has.
- **Issuing (4.7):** the map image prints on issued sheets too. It is not part of the fingerprint: it comes from
  tiles, not from what a person edits; the place that centres it already counts. Don't add a recipe version.
- **Credits:** the footer's OpenStreetMap credit (4.6) stays; with an own provider, the footer carries that
  provider's credit when its tiles printed.

## Files

- src/features/callsheets/ (the map in the location block; callSheetPdf.ts takes the composed map per location as
  input, so it stays pure and testable; exportCallSheet.ts/issueCallSheet.ts gather the tiles first);
  src/app/mapTiles.ts if a batch call helps; src/core/i18n/locales/en.ts and es.ts.
- e2e/callSheets.spec.ts.

## Tests to add

- Vitest: the tiles needed for a place at zoom 16 in a 60 × 40 mm frame (count ≤ 6, right x/y, the antimeridian);
  the PDF with a map input (image or grid drawn inside the frame, nothing outside it, the credit under it, the pin at
  the place); the PDF without tiles (empty frame and the line); an issued PDF still prints it; the fingerprint
  unchanged by the map.
- Playwright (Chromium): the Call sheet tile shows the location's map (browser grid) with its credit; Export PDF
  holds the map frame and credit text (and the grid's marker you choose to identify it).
