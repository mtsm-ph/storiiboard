   is a symbol or a letter draws as a true-size outline with the symbol in the middle". 6.17 draws the outline
   round every `symbol` icon item, including signs whose size is one number, their longer side (iconSizes.ts
   `STATED_SIZES` `{ side }`, 6.6's `isSymbolSize`): their box already has the drawing's own proportions, so they

   and a depth (a footprint: Parking, Basecamp, Video village, Generator and the like). A sign sized by its side
   draws exactly as it did before 6.17 (no outline, no margin), so plans already in files look as they did.
   Make the two tables agree and say so in one place: an icon sized by `{ side }` is never treated as a footprint;
   `ICON_CLASSES` stays the one explicit table, with a test that every `symbol` with a width and depth gets the
   outline and no `{ side }` icon does. Fix the stale `SymbolShape` mention near iconSizes.ts:162.
