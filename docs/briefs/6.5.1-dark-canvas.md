## What to build

1. **The setting.** In src/core/storage/settings.ts: `canvasView: { plan: 'paper' | 'dark'; board: 'paper' | 'dark' }`,
   default paper for both, read defensively as the other settings are (anything else reads as paper). One choice
   for every plan on this computer and one for every board and deck (a deck is a board). Never a prop, never an
   op, never in History, never in a .sbd.
2. **The toggle.** On each canvas, in the cluster where its zoom control sits: a two-state control, "Paper" and
   "Dark" (one button that says the view it switches to is fine if it reads clearly; it needs an accessible name,
   `aria-pressed` or a radio pair, keyboard reachable, a tooltip). Words through t()/tn() in en and es
   ("Papel", "Oscuro"). Changing it is not a project edit: ⌘Z never undoes it, and it must not mark the project
   changed, write a backup or touch the "changed" state.
3. **What Dark draws.**
   - The canvas background: the dark theme's page colour (the abyssal token), with no paper grain if grain is drawn.
   - Anything with NO colour of its own draws in the light ink (the dark theme's text colour, paper): default
     text, a wall's fill and outline, a door's and window's lines, default outlines of shapes and frames, default
     connector lines, the kit's icons' ink strokes, labels (camera letters, heights, watts, mark numbers), the
     scale bar, north, the sun path's own ink, the measure, selection and hover outlines, handles, snap guides,
     the plan's and board's grid if one is drawn, the page frames of a deck and their names.
   - Where the default drawing is an ink FILL with paper text on it (or paper halo behind a label), the pair
     swaps together, so contrast is kept: no paper-on-paper, no ink-on-dark.
   - Unchanged in Dark: any colour the person chose (text colour, a note's or sticky's colour, a shape's fill or
     stroke, a connector's colour), even one equal to ink; pictures and video stills; the underlay picture;
     department colours and tags; the accent; cones' and beams' tints if they are colours of their own (if a cone
     or beam is drawn as ink at an opacity, it follows the ink swap). A sticky or note with a chosen or default
     swatch keeps its fill, and its text stays whatever is readable on THAT fill, as in Paper.
   - Shot and scene cards on a board are app surfaces drawn on the board: keep them as they are in Paper unless
     they already follow the app's theme; say what you found.
   - Dimmed layers stay dimmed by the one dim.
4. **How.** One small palette object (`paper` or `dark`: background, ink, inkSoft/halo, whatever the drawing
   needs; in src/ui/canvas, its hexes read from the design tokens, not typed into components) passed from the
   host into the drawing functions. No colour stored in a record is ever rewritten; "no colour of its own" is
   decided where the drawing reads the record (the existing "a missing colour is ink" rule), never by comparing a
   stored colour with ink. The canvas keeps an item's drawing while its `draw` function is the same (ItemNodes):
   the host must hand a new `draw` when the view changes, so nothing stays stale after the toggle. A board that
   passes nothing behaves exactly as before.
5. **Never in Dark:** every export and print path (src/features/export: storyboard, plan PDF and PNG, department
   prints, deck and board PDF and PNG, the call sheet's map), thumbnails and previews drawn off the board (board
   lists, cards of boards, the Frames tile), the published/review copies later. They take the paper palette
   explicitly, whatever the setting says. The location map (LocationMap.tsx) is not a board or plan: untouched.
6. **The app's theme is separate.** The app in light with the canvas in Dark, and the app in dark with the canvas
   in Paper, both work. Don't tie the default to the theme.
