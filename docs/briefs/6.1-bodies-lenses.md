## Goal

Gear can hold camera bodies (with the sensor area of the recording mode used) and lenses (focal range, squeeze). A
frame can pick its lens. One function works out the horizontal field of view the project's frame sees. The file
format gets the one schema version Phase 6 needs. No plan is drawn yet.

## 2. The field of view: one function, src/core/model/fieldOfView.ts (+ test)

Kevin's rule (decision 6): the cone shows what the project's frame sees, not the whole sensor.

- Inputs: the mode's `sensor_width_mm` and `sensor_height_mm`, `squeeze` (1 by default), the focal length in mm,
  the project's frame shape (the project's `aspect_ratio`; find how the project stores it and parse it in one
  place: "2.39:1", "16:9", "9:16", a decimal), and `onSide` (false by default).
- Steps: if `onSide`, swap the mode's width and height. The de-squeezed area is (width × squeeze) by height. Fit
  the frame shape inside it (the largest rectangle of that shape that fits). The result's width is the width used:
  horizontal field of view = 2 × atan(width / (2 × focal length)), in degrees. Return both the width in mm and the
  angle. Missing or non-positive inputs return null, never NaN.
- Tests that must be in the file, on a 35.6 × 20.0 mm mode:
  - 50 mm, 2× squeeze, 2.39:1 frame: 47.8 mm wide, 51.1° (and explicitly not 70.9°).
  - 24 mm, no squeeze, 9:16 frame: 11.25 mm, 26.4°.
  - the same on its side: 20.0 mm, 45.2°.
  Round only for display (one decimal); compare in tests to within 0.05.
- Nothing draws it yet (6.4), but the Inspector shows it: see 4.

## 3. Bodies and lenses in Gear (the Gear tile, 3.9a; src/features/gear, src/core/model for `gear_item`)

- A gear item has a kind: Gear (as today), Camera body, Lens. Existing items are untouched and read as Gear.
- Camera body: a "Camera and recording mode" choice filled from the table below, which sets the mode's name, width
  and height; all three stay editable by hand afterwards (typing over them keeps the typed values). A letter (A, B,
  C…), unique among the project's bodies, offered in order. The project's first body by letter is "the default
  body" (a function in the model; 6.4 uses it).
- Lens: prime or zoom (one focal length, or from and to, in mm, from ≤ to), and squeeze: 1 (spherical), 1.33, 1.5,
  1.8, 2, or typed (between 1 and 2.5).

## 4. A frame picks its lens (the Inspector, src/features/storyboard/InspectorView.tsx)

- Next to the lens field: a Lens choice listing the project's lenses ("35 mm", "24–70 mm", with "2×" when the
  squeeze isn't 1) and "No lens picked". Picking a prime sets `lens_id` and `lens_mm` in one op group; picking a
  zoom sets `lens_id` and keeps `lens_mm` if it is inside the range, else sets it to the nearest end. Typing a
  `lens_mm` outside the picked lens's range is refused in the field with a short message (the range), never saved.
  Deleting (trashing) a lens leaves frames' `lens_id` in place; a trashed or missing lens reads as none picked,
  squeeze 1, through one reader function, as 6.0 did for setups.
- Under it, read only, mono: the field of view for this frame with the project's default body, for example
  "51.1° · 47.8 mm of 35.6 × 20.0" ; with no body in the project, a muted line saying to add a camera body in Gear.
- Do not change what `lens_mm` means anywhere else (exports, shot list, checks, approvals' fingerprint).
