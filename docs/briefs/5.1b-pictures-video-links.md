# Brief 5.1b: pictures, video cards and links on a board

For the builder (.claude/agents/builder.md). Written by the main session, Nov 11, 2026. Read CLAUDE.md first; its
Safety section comes before this brief. You start with no other context: everything you need is here or in the files
named.

## Goal

The second half of 5.1 (docs/PLAN.md, "Phase 5 milestones"; Kevin's answers in docs/PHASE5_DECISIONS.md, decisions 4
and 5): pictures dropped, pasted or chosen onto a board, video cards with a still that open the Player, and link cards.
Builds on 5.0 and 5.1a (read docs/briefs/5.0-canvas-core.md and 5.1a-text-shapes-fonts.md, then src/ui/canvas/ and
src/features/moodboards/).

## Decisions that apply

- **Pictures are stored as frames' are** (decision 4; Kevin, Nov 10): at most 2560 px on the long edge plus the 640 px
  thumbnail, in `media_files` by SHA-256, with a pointer to the original (`source_path`, relative to the .sbd when it
  can be), and **never the original embedded**. Reuse the frames' pipeline as it is (src/core/media/image.ts and
  store.ts, src/app/pictures.ts, src/features/storyboard/addPictures.ts and useBoardDrops.ts for how drops and pastes
  arrive in the app and the browser build); don't fork it. A pasted picture has no original, as on frames.
  - The record shape: a `board_item` of kind `picture` (geometry as other items; its size keeps the picture's aspect,
    long edge 400 units when placed) with its `media` record (kind `image`) as its child, as a frame's media is its
    shot's child. Trash, restore, Empty Trash and Clean up old pictures treat it as they treat a frame's picture (find
    how purge decides a picture's bytes are unused and make sure board pictures count as users).
  - Copy and paste, and duplicate, of a picture item make a new item and a new media record naming the same stored file
    (no second copy of the bytes).
  - Several files dropped at once land side by side, left to right from the drop point, in one op group.
- **Drawing pictures** (decision 4): the canvas draws the 640 px thumbnail while the item is drawn at 640 screen px or
  less on its long edge, and the 2560 px picture only up close; images load lazily (only items in view), decoded once
  and kept in a bounded cache (drop the least recently drawn beyond about 64 full pictures). A picture whose bytes are
  gone (deleted for good) draws a placeholder that says so.
- **Video cards** (decision 5): a video file dropped or chosen goes in as frames' videos do (src/features/video/
  addVideos.ts, linkVideos.ts: linked by path, never copied in; its poster still; its playback copy made with the
  frames' pipeline). The item is a `board_item` of kind `video` with its `media` (kind `video`) as its child. The card
  draws the poster, the file name and the duration (timecode in JetBrains Mono). A missing file is flagged on the card
  with Relink, as frames do (CLAUDE.md, Missing media).
  - Double-click or Enter on a video card opens the Player tile in the current workspace playing that video. The
    Player today plays the selected frame's clip (src/features/video/PlayerTile.tsx): add the smallest way for it to
    play a board card's video instead (a "playing" source of either kind). For a card, frame stepping, play and pause
    work; I and O, grab a still and the frame's clip tools are for frames only: hidden or disabled with a short line
    saying they work on a storyboard frame.
  - No playback on the canvas itself.
- **Link cards:** a `board_item` of kind `link` with `url` (http or https only, validated) and an optional `title` the
  person types. The card shows the title or the host and path. Open goes through the app's opener (src/app/opener.ts →
  src-tauri/src/open.rs); never fetch the page, a title or a preview (no network host is allowed for it). Tool L
  places one and asks for the address; pasting text that is one http(s) address onto the focused canvas makes a link
  card at the pointer.
- **Tools and strip:** a Picture button (choose files, the app's picture dialog), the Link tool, and Choose video. The
  properties strip shows, for a picture, its original's name and "Show original" (the opener reveals the file when it
  exists; says so when it's missing); for a link, its address and title, editable.
- Words through t()/tn() in en and es; History and the Trash name the new kinds ("Picture on Camera board").
- SBD_FORMAT.md: the three kinds' props and their media children, in the Boards section and the Change log; no
  migration, no schema_version change.

## Tests to add

- Vitest: a dropped picture makes one item and its media child in one group, sized to the aspect; three dropped land
  side by side; paste of a picture item reuses the stored file; trash and restore with the media; Empty Trash keeps
  bytes another frame or item still uses and removes them otherwise; thumbnail vs full by drawn size; the cache bound;
  a link's URL validation (http/https only, javascript: and file: refused); pasted text that's a URL makes a link; a
  video card's open sets the Player's source; History and Trash words.
- Playwright (Chromium, `?files=memory`): drop or paste a picture on a board (the browser build's own drag and paste
  events, as e2e/pictures.spec.ts does for frames), reload, still there; zoom in past the threshold and the full
  picture is drawn (a test hook saying which size is drawn); a video card from a fixture file (as e2e/video.spec.ts
  does) opens the Player and plays; a link card made with L and by pasting a URL; delete and restore a picture item;
  axe on a board with all three kinds, light and dark. No console errors.

## Checks to run

- `npx playwright test --project=chromium`: alone, never two at once, wait for it to finish; then `npm run
  failed-tests` and report every failed test's name with the passed and failed counts, for every full run.
