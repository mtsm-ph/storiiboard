# Brief 6.19c: a selected wall can be moved by its body

Read CLAUDE.md first. Found while fixing the Mac's failures (6.19b): on a plan at 100%, a selected wall is about
10 px thick and its selection frame (the canvas core's Konva Transformer) puts 8 px resize anchors on it, among
them one at the middle of each long side. A press on the wall's centre line, meaning to move it, lands on the
`bottom-center` or `top-center` anchor more often than on the wall: the drag resizes (or does nothing) instead of
moving. Which it does depends on a fraction of a pixel, so it differs between engines: it is very likely the one
plan.spec.ts test still failing in WebKit on the Mac ("starts a room, draws walls by click and typed length…"),
and the reason two plan tests failed in Chromium when the canvas's centre was made a whole pixel.

**The app fix.** A person must be able to grab a selected wall by its body and move it, at any zoom at which the
wall can be picked at all. Read how plan items get their Transformer (src/ui/canvas and
src/features/birdseye/plan: wallShape.ts, planItems.ts, the selection frame's set-up) and how 6.5's review handled
thin frames ("A thin frame keeps its depth through a turn and can be picked and turned at any zoom": follow that
precedent). Decide and build the smallest of:
- a wall (and any item whose shown thickness is under about three anchor sizes) gets no anchors on its long
  sides, only at its ends (a wall's thickness is a typed field, never dragged, if that is how it is today:
  check), or
- anchors never take a press that falls on the item's own body when the item is thinner than the anchor is
  wide: the body wins and the press moves the item.
Whichever you choose: resizing a wall's length by its end anchors, turning it, typing its length and thickness,
doors and windows following it, undo, and every other item's handles stay exactly as they are. Say which you
chose and why.

**Tests.** Vitest or Chromium, new: at 100% and at the smallest zoom where a wall can still be picked, a press at
the middle of a selected wall's centre line and a drag of 1 m moves the wall 1 m (start and end both), and its
length is unchanged; the same pressing half a pixel either side of the centre line. Then run e2e/plan.spec.ts with
`wholePixelCentre` (e2e/wholePixels.ts, added in 6.19b; see how planUnderlay.spec.ts calls it inside `screen()`
with `{ corner: false, tries: 160 }`) added to plan.spec.ts's own positioning helper the same way: with the app
fix both tests that failed with it ("starts a room…" and "puts a door and a window in a wall…") must pass, with
every assertion word for word. If they still fail, leave plan.spec.ts as it is at HEAD and report what you saw.

Checks: `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npx eslint .`, `npx vitest run`, e2e/plan*.spec.ts and e2e/library.spec.ts in
Chromium. Report what you chose, the tests, counts.
