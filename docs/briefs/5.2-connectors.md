# Brief 5.2: connectors

For the builder (.claude/agents/builder.md). Written by the main session, Nov 11, 2026. Read CLAUDE.md first; its
Safety section comes before this brief. You start with no other context: everything you need is here or in the files
named.

## Goal

Milestone 5.2 (docs/PLAN.md, "Phase 5 milestones"; the scope in "Phase 5 notes": "connectors that snap to items and
move with them (straight, bent or curved, with arrowheads and labels), and quick-create of a connected item"). Builds
on 5.0, 5.1a and 5.1b: read docs/briefs/5.0-canvas-core.md, 5.1a-text-shapes-fonts.md, 5.1b-pictures-video-links.md,
then src/ui/canvas/ and src/features/moodboards/.

## Decisions that apply

- **The record:** a `board_item` of kind `connector` under its board (props in docs/SBD_FORMAT.md's Boards section
  and Change log; no migration, no schema_version change):
  - `from` and `to`: each either `{ item: <board_item id>, side: 'top' | 'right' | 'bottom' | 'left' | 'auto' }`
    (attached) or `{ x, y }` (a free end, world units). `auto` picks the side facing the other end each time it's drawn.
  - `route`: `straight`, `elbow` (right angles only) or `curved`; `start_head` and `end_head`: `none` or `arrow`
    (default `none` → `arrow`); `color` (`#RRGGBB`, missing = ink, as 5.1a's rule: board content is never themed);
    `width` (world units, default 1.5); `label` (text, may be empty), drawn at the path's middle on a paper patch.
  - A connector has no x, y, width or height of its own: its path is worked out when drawn from its ends. An attached
    end follows its item live; moving, resizing or rotating an item writes nothing to its connectors.
  - Z order is sibling order like every item; a new connector goes to the back of the board's order so it draws
    under the items it joins.
- **Drawing a connector:** the Connector tool (C) in the tool strip: drag from an item (or empty space) to an item (or
  empty space). Near an item while dragging (within 12 screen px of its box), the end snaps to that item, to the side
  nearest the pointer, with the side's midpoint shown; over the item's middle third it attaches `auto`. Escape
  cancels. After drawing, the tool goes back to Select and the connector is selected.
- **Editing:** a selected connector shows its two end handles (and, for elbow and curved, nothing else to drag in
  5.2). Dragging an end re-attaches or frees it, with the same snapping. Double-click or Enter edits its label in
  place (5.1a's text editor). The properties strip shows route, the two heads, colour, width and label.
- **Hit testing:** a connector is picked within 6 screen px of its path (straight segments, elbow segments, the
  curve sampled); a marquee selects it when it touches its path. Tab reaches connectors in z order like other items.
- **Routing**, pure and tested in src/ui/canvas/ (the core stays board-agnostic: it routes between boxes and points):
  - straight: from the end's anchor to the other's;
  - elbow: leaves each attached end perpendicular to its side, at least 16 units out, then right angles, at most
    four segments; between two free ends, a single bend;
  - curved: a cubic Bézier whose control points leave each attached end perpendicular to its side by a third of the
    distance (a free end aims at the other end);
  - rotated items: anchors are the rotated side midpoints, and "perpendicular" is the rotated side's normal.
  - Arrowheads sit at the very end and point along the last segment's direction.
- **Trash:** trashing an item also trashes the connectors attached to it, in the same op group. The Trash doesn't
  list connectors on their own (as sketch strokes aren't listed): restoring an item from the Trash also restores the
  connectors trashed with it whose other end is live or free. A connector the person selected and deleted is listed
  ("Connector on Camera board") and restored alone; if one of its items is trashed, it stays hidden until that item
  is back. Views skip a connector with a trashed or missing end. Empty Trash purges a trashed item's trashed
  connectors with it.
- **Copy and paste:** a connector whose two attached items are both copied is pasted attached to their copies; a
  connector copied without one of its items pastes that end as a free end at its current place. The 5.1b clipboard
  rules apply (human text for the system clipboard: the label, if any).
- **Quick-create:** with exactly one sticky, shape or text box selected, four small square "+" handles
  show just outside its sides. Clicking one (or ⌘⌥ + an arrow key, Ctrl+Alt on Windows) creates a copy of the item's
  kind and style (empty text), 80 units beyond that side, aligned to it, with a connector from the original's side to
  the new item's facing side, in one op group, then starts editing the new item's text when it has text. Dragging a
  "+" handle draws a connector instead, as the Connector tool does.
- **History** names connector steps ("Added a connector", "Connected 2 items", "Changed a connector's label"); every
  word through t()/tn(), en and es. Accessibility: a selected connector is announced with what it joins ("Connector
  from Sticky to Rectangle"); the "+" handles are reachable from the keyboard as the arrow shortcut, and said in the
  canvas's help text.

## Tests to add

- Vitest: routing (straight, elbow and curved between two boxes on each pair of sides, rotated boxes, free ends, the
  16-unit stand-off, at most four elbow segments); arrowhead direction; hit test on each route; snapping (side picked
  by pointer, `auto` in the middle third); moving an item writes no connector op; trashing an item trashes its
  connectors in one group, undo restores both; Trash restore of the item brings its connectors back; a connector
  deleted on its own is listed and restored; Empty Trash purges them together; copy with and without its items;
  quick-create makes item and connector in one group; History words.
- Playwright (Chromium, `?files=memory`): draw a connector between two stickies with C, move one sticky, the
  connector follows (read the path from a test hook); switch route to elbow and curved; add an arrowhead and a label;
  drag an end to a third item; quick-create with "+" and with ⌘⌥→; delete a sticky, the connector goes, Restore
  brings both back; keyboard-only: select an item, ⌘⌥↓, type, Escape; axe light and dark. No console errors.
