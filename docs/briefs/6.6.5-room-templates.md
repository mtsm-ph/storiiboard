## What to build

1. **A room template** is a plain value: a name and the room's own things in metres about their own centre:
   walls (with thickness), doors and windows (in their wall, with width, and sill/top or height where set), set
   pieces (kind, icon, size, turn, colour, label), and the ceiling height if set. Never: the underlay or its
   picture, north, the sun path, cameras, lights, grip, sound, production items, marks, anything of a setup or
   of blocking, gear links, ids of other records, file paths, or anything naming a person or a project.
2. **"Save room as a template…"** in the room's menu (where "Make a separate copy" lives): asks a name (cleaned
   as a workspace's name is, at most 60 characters), saves the current room's own level as a template ON THIS
   COMPUTER. Not an op; nothing is written to the project.
3. **Where templates live:** on this computer, as company profiles are owned (the person's, not the project's),
   but in an app data file through appData.ts (or its browser-build stand-in), not in the settings blob: a room
   can be large and localStorage is small. One file, a list of templates, at most 200, each at most 256 KB as
   JSON. Read defensively (the 6.5.8 pattern): bounded BEFORE walking, rebuilt clean (only known kinds and
   icons; numbers finite and within the plan's own limits; at most 500 walls, 500 openings, 1,000 set pieces;
   an opening whose wall isn't in the template is dropped; a kind this version doesn't know is dropped and
   named when placing). If templates must live in localStorage instead (no app data path in the browser build),
   its key joins the right list in settingsPreset.test's storage-key walk (NEVER_IN_PRESET or NOT_A_SETTING,
   with the reason): say which.
4. **Managing them:** the Library tile gets a "Rooms" group (in both sorts) listing "My templates" by name, each
   with Rename and Delete (Delete asks, since templates have no Trash; undoable for a few seconds through the
   app's notice if the notice supports an action, else a confirm). Built-in styled rooms will be listed here
   too, later; leave room in the code for a read-only list, empty for now.
5. **Placing one copies it into the project:** drag it from the Library onto the plan (or tap, or Enter for the
   middle of what is seen), with the whole room's outline following the pointer; release writes every wall,
   opening and set piece as NEW records into the scene's room plan (new ids; the level each kind goes to today;
   ceiling height only if the room has none) in ONE op group, one undo; the placed things are selected so the
   person can move or turn them together. A second place of the same template makes separate records: nothing
   links back to the template ("copies it into the project"). Snapping: the template's corners snap to the
   plan's walls and snap points as a wall's ends do. A deferred write carries the project it began in (6.4).
   Locked or hidden art layer: refused with the reason.
6. **The five styled rooms, as PICTURES ONLY.** Write them as five template values in a file under
   test-results/styled-rooms/ (not in src; they are proposals), each built only from what the kit has (walls,
   doors, windows and the nine set pieces: bed, car, chair, column, counter-bar, plant-tree, sofa, stairs, table)
   at the kit's real sizes, and draw each through the plan's own PNG export (a probe spec or script under
   test-results/build665/ that makes a project, places the template with item 5's function, and exports the PNG
   at a round scale with the legend), to test-results/styled-rooms/living-room.png, kitchen.png, bedroom.png,
   office.png, bar.png. Compositions (inner sizes; adjust a piece's place so nothing overlaps and doors open
   clear):
   - **Living room, 5.0 × 4.0 m:** a door in the bottom wall near the left corner; a 2 m window centred in the
     top wall; a sofa against the left wall facing right; a low table in front of it; two chairs across the
     table, turned to face the sofa; a plant in the top-right corner.
   - **Kitchen, 4.0 × 3.0 m:** a door in the left wall; a 1.2 m window centred in the top wall; a counter along
     the top wall under the window and a second counter along the right wall (an L); a table in the lower left
     with four chairs.
   - **Bedroom, 4.0 × 3.5 m:** a door in the bottom wall near the right corner; a 1.5 m window in the left wall;
     a bed with its head against the top wall, centred; a table as a desk under the window with one chair; a
     plant in the bottom-left corner.
   - **Office, 4.0 × 3.0 m:** a door in the bottom wall near the left; a 1.8 m window in the top wall; a table
     as a desk in the upper half with a chair behind it and two chairs in front; a sofa against the right wall;
     a plant by the door.
   - **Bar, 7.0 × 5.0 m:** a 1.2 m door centred in the bottom wall; two 1.5 m windows in the bottom wall either
     side of it; a long counter along the top wall with five chairs as stools on the room's side; three tables
     in the lower half, each with four chairs; two columns in the middle third.
   Also write test-results/styled-rooms/README.md listing, per room, each piece and its size, and anything the
   kit lacks that the room wants (a fridge, a stove, a sink, a wardrobe, a nightstand, a desk lamp, shelves, a
   TV, a rug, bar stools): Kevin decides whether to draw them.
