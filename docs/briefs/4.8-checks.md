# Brief 4.8: call sheet checks

For the builder (.claude/agents/builder.md). Written by the main session, Nov 10, 2026. Read CLAUDE.md first; its
Safety section comes before this brief. You start with no other context: everything you need is here or in the files
named.

## Goal

The call sheet flags what's likely wrong before it goes out, with checks shared by the app and (from Phase 7) the MCP
server (CLAUDE.md, "MCP server: Checks"; docs/PLAN.md, 4.8). The pattern is 3.9b's first check,
src/core/checks/gearOnDay.ts (pure, live records only, cached per records object, tested; shown in the Shoot days
tile), exported from src/core/checks/index.ts. Read it, docs/briefs/4.1-call-sheet.md to 4.7-issuing.md, and the call
sheet's code (src/core/model/callsheet.ts, src/features/callsheets/) first.

## The checks (each its own file in src/core/checks, each finding a small typed object with a `kind`)

1. **Location missing details** (`location-missing`): a live location on a day's sheet without an address, a contact
   (name or phone), or a parking note; one finding per location per day, listing which are missing.
2. **Department missing** (`department-missing`): a department a day's scenes need with nobody from it on the day
   (4.2's people on the day). Rules, in code, each with a comment:
   - Sound when any of the day's scenes has dialogue: the scene has at least one character (3.13's character_ids,
     from the script's speaking characters), or any of its frames has dialogue text (read src/core/model/storyboard.ts
     for the frame's dialogue field).
   - HMU and Wardrobe when any cast member is on the day.
   - Camera always (a day with frames to shoot).
   - Direction always.
   Days with nobody chosen yet (4.2: no `people_ids`, everyone counts) still run the check against everyone.
3. **HMU too short** (`hmu-too-short`): a cast member on the day whose HMU ready time is less than a set number of
   minutes after their call (both from 4.2's person times; call worked out as 4.2 does). The minimum is a project prop
   `hmu_minutes` (default 45, 0–600), editable on the call sheet's Cast section heading ("HMU needs at least 45 min").
   Also flag HMU ready after on-set time. Times past midnight: treat a time earlier than the call by more than 12 h as
   the next day.
4. **Golden-hour scene at midday** (`golden-hour-midday`): a scene on the day whose DAY/NIGHT is DAWN, DUSK or MAGIC
   HOUR (storyboard.ts DAY_NIGHT) when the day's working hours (crew call to wrap; without a wrap, crew call plus
   3.7's `dayTotals` minutes) don't overlap the location's golden hour or blue hour that day (4.3's sunTimes). The data
   has no time per scene, so the day's hours stand in for it; say so in the finding's words ("not during today's
   hours"). Without a place, a date or a crew call: no finding.
   The finding names the scene and the golden hour's times.
5. **Exterior with rain** (`exterior-rain`): an EXT or INT/EXT scene on a day whose forecast (4.4: src/core/net/weather.ts's day summary and its precipitation spans; src/features/callsheets/forecast.ts's held forecasts) at its location
   gives precipitation ≥ 1 mm (or its US-units equivalent shown) during the day's hours (crew call to wrap, else the
   whole day), or a rain/thunder/sleet/snow condition at the call. Only from a forecast already held (never fetch for
   a check); no forecast: no finding. Because the forecast isn't in the records, this check takes the held forecasts
   as an argument (the pure function gets them; the MCP will pass none until it has its own).

## How they show

- On the call sheet: a "Checks" strip under the top row, listing each finding in words (en and es) with the warning
  style (Truffle, ▲), each linking to where it's fixed when that's on the sheet (e.g. the location block, the Cast
  heading) by moving focus there. "No problems found" when clear.
- In the Shoot days tile, beside 3.9b's gear check, a count "3 checks" for the day, opening the call sheet.
- The PDF (4.6) never prints the checks.
- Issuing (4.7: src/features/callsheets/IssueBar.tsx and issueCallSheet.ts) with findings asks once: "Issue with 3 problems?" (Issue anyway / Cancel), through the app's ask
  dialog (src/app/ask.tsx), never a browser confirm.

## Files

- src/core/checks/: one file per check plus tests; index.ts exports them and a `callSheetChecks(records, projectId,
  dayId, forecasts?)` that runs them for one day.
- src/features/callsheets/ (the strip; the Cast heading's HMU minutes field and its edit, one op); the Issue flow;
  src/features/schedule/DaysTile.tsx (the count); src/features/history/summary.ts for `hmu_minutes`;
  src/core/i18n/locales/en.ts and es.ts; docs/SBD_FORMAT.md (`hmu_minutes` on the project, additive).
- e2e/callSheets.spec.ts.

## Tests to add

- Vitest for each check: found and not found, trashed records ignored, edge cases (missing times, overnight times,
  no place, no forecast, no people chosen), the default and a set hmu_minutes; callSheetChecks for one day.
- Playwright (Chromium): a day with a location missing parking shows the finding; filling it clears it; a cast
  member with HMU 20 min after call shows "HMU too short"; Issue with problems asks and Cancel issues nothing.
