# Brief 6.4: cameras

Phase 6, milestone 6.4 (docs/PLAN.md; docs/PHASE6_DECISIONS.md decisions 4, 5, 6, 11). Read CLAUDE.md first (its
"Bird's-eye view" rules), then those decisions, then the briefs of what is built: docs/briefs/6.1-bodies-lenses.md
(src/core/model/fieldOfView.ts, cameraGear.ts: `defaultBody`, `frameLens`), 6.2a, 6.2b and 6.3 (the plan host, the
Library, icon items), and docs/briefs/canvas-core-map.md. Stay on the branch checked out.

## Goal

A camera can be placed on a setup's plan. It knows its body and the frames shot from it, and draws the field of
view of the picked frame's lens, live. The Frames tile under the plan lists the setup's frames. The lens check
joins src/core/checks.

## Decisions that apply

- A camera is a `plan_item` of `kind: 'camera'` in the setup's plan (never the room's), with `icon` (which camera
  icon it was placed as: `camera/camera`, `sticks`, `dolly-track`, `slider`, `jib-crane`, `gimbal`, `handheld`,
  `steadicam`, `drone`), geometry as every item (its rotation is where it points), and:
  - `body_id`: the `gear_item` body it is. Absent: the project's default body, read live (`defaultBody`).
  - `shot_ids`: the frames shot from it. Only frames of this setup (`frameSetupId`) count; an id that is trashed,
    gone or in another setup reads as not linked (one reader). A frame is linked to at most one camera of its
    setup: linking it to another unlinks it from the first in the same op group.
  - `shown_shot_id`: the linked frame whose lens the cone draws. Absent or not linked: the first linked frame in
    board order.
  - `lens_mm` and `lens_id`: only meaningful while the camera has no linked frame (a lens typed or picked on it).
  - `on_side`: true for vertical shooting.
  - `height_m`: its height when set. With a linked frame that has a `height` (3.18; find how frames store it and
    show that, read live, never copied) the frame's wins and the camera's field is read-only with a note.
- **Live from the frame, never a copy** (decision 6): with a shown frame, the cone uses that frame's `lens_mm`
  and its picked lens's squeeze (`frameLens`), the camera's body's mode, the project's `aspect_ratio` and
  `on_side`, through `fieldOfView`. Change the lens on the board and the cone follows with no edit to the plan.
- **The cone**: from the camera's centre along its rotation, the horizontal field of view wide, drawn 6 m long
  (a constant; no new prop), its far edge open. Plans are never themed (decision 13): the cone is ink at about 8%
  fill with thin ink edges, in light and dark; selected, the edges are full ink. (design/DESIGN.md's older line
  about an accent cone is superseded by decision 13; say so in a comment.) A mono label by the camera: its letter,
  focal length and angle ("A · 50 mm · 51.1°"); with no body or no focal length the label says what is missing and
  no cone is drawn. A set height shows as a small mono label by the item ("1.5 m" or 4' 11").
- The cone reaches outside the item's box: the canvas core culls by item bounds, so give `CanvasItem` an optional
  extra draw extent (the smallest option that keeps a cone visible while its camera's box is off screen and keeps
  picking on the box), optional so boards are unchanged.
- **Copies drop frame links**: "Start from another setup" and any other copy of a camera (paste, duplicate, the
  separate copy) leave out `shot_ids` and `shown_shot_id`, so two cameras never claim one frame.
- **The lens check** (CLAUDE.md, "Storyboard lens differs from the camera lens in the bird's-eye plan"), in
  src/core/checks with the others and its own test: a camera that carries a typed `lens_mm` (set while it had no
  frame) and now has a linked frame whose `lens_mm` differs is a finding naming the scene, setup, frame and both
  lenses. The Selection tile shows it on the camera with a button "Use the frame's lens" that clears the typed
  lens (one op). A camera with no typed lens can never disagree.
- Nothing here changes a frame's props except linking (which lives on the camera), so approvals and issued call
  sheets are untouched: add a test that they are.
- No new dependency, table, record type or schema version. Any prop beyond docs/SBD_FORMAT.md's schema 14 list is
  added there and named in the report.

## What to build

1. **Model** (src/core/model/planCamera.ts + test): camera fields, the readers above (linked frames, shown frame,
   body, lens and squeeze, height), the cone's numbers for a camera, link, unlink and show edits as op lists.
2. **Library**: the camera group's icons are placeable now (not `camera/field-of-view`, which stays a picture of
   the cone and is not an item). Default sizes in iconSizes.ts, metres: camera 0.4 × 0.4; sticks 1.0 × 1.0;
   dolly-track 1.0 × 3.0; slider 1.2 × 0.3; jib-crane 1.5 × 4.0; gimbal 0.5 × 0.5; handheld 0.6 × 0.6; steadicam
   0.7 × 0.7; drone 0.6 × 0.6. A key for the camera tool: C if free in the plan's tools, else say which.
3. **Drawing** in the plan host: the icon, the cone, the labels; a rotate handle that turns the camera (the core's);
   arrow keys nudge; `[` and `]` turn it 5° (Shift: 1°) if those keys are free.
4. **The Selection tile** for a camera: body (the project's bodies by letter and name, "Default body"), its
   frames (linked, with the shown one marked; link and unlink), lens (read-only from the shown frame with a
   "Change on the board" link that selects that frame on the storyboard; or the typed lens and Lens choice when it
   has no frame, validated as the Inspector does), On its side, height, rotation, X and Y, and the worked-out
   line "51.1° · 47.8 mm of 35.6 × 20.0" as the Inspector shows it.
5. **The Frames tile** (workspace tile id `frames`; map it in tileContent.tsx): the shown setup's frames in board
   order, each with its picture, shot number (mono), size, lens, and which camera shoots it (a choice of the
   setup's cameras by letter, or none). Pressing a frame makes it its camera's shown frame and selects that camera
   on the plan; a button opens the frame on the storyboard. States in words: no setup shown; no frames in this
   setup (point at the Setups tile); no cameras yet.
6. History lines (en and es): camera placed, turned, its body changed, frame linked, unlinked, shown, set on its
   side, lens typed, "now uses the frame's lens". Trash, export round trip.
