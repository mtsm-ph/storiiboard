# Brief 4.2: who's on the day, and when

For the builder (.claude/agents/builder.md). Written by the main session, Nov 10, 2026. Read CLAUDE.md first; its
Safety section comes before this brief. You start with no other context: everything you need is here or in the files
named.

## Goal

4.1 built the Call sheet tile (src/features/callsheets/CallSheetTile.tsx and sections.tsx; the data from
`callSheet(records, projectId, dayId, people?)` in src/core/model/callsheet.ts; edits in callsheetEdits.ts): a shoot
day's call sheet, live from the project, with notes of its own on a `callsheet` record under the day (`dayCallsheet`).
Read those files and docs/briefs/4.1-call-sheet.md first. 4.2 says who is on the day and when
each of them is called (docs/PHASE4_DECISIONS.md, decisions 6 and 9; CLAUDE.md, "Call sheets"):
- only the people on the day are on its call sheet, with "Add everyone" for a small crew;
- calls are overrides of the day's crew call, per department and per person;
- cast have a call, HMU ready and on-set time;
- phone and email print only where the person's record allows it: on by default for crew, off for cast.

## Decisions that apply

- **Who's on the day: `people_ids` on the `shoot_day`** (person ids in the order added, each once). It's a fact about
  the day, read by the Calendar's "1 of 3 confirmed" and the Shoot days tile's confirm list as well as the call sheet.
  - A day without the prop (every day made before 4.2) reads as everyone live in Cast and crew, as it does today, so
    nothing changes on an existing project until someone chooses (Kevin, Nov 9: no change to existing data without
    him). The call sheet then says "Everyone in Cast and crew" with a button "Choose who's on the day", which writes the
    list as everyone live now, in the order Cast and crew lists them, as one edit.
  - A day made from 4.2 on (src/features/schedule/dayEdits.ts `newDay`) starts with `people_ids: []`: nobody yet.
  - Edits, each one op group (one ⌘Z): add a person; take a person off; Add everyone (adds every live person not yet
    on, in Cast and crew's order, after those already there). Taking someone off leaves their `crew` confirmation and
    their times as they are (so adding them back brings them back), but they read as off the day everywhere.
  - Ids of people in the Trash or gone are kept in the list as they are (a restore brings them back) and skipped when
    read. Odd values (not a list, non-strings) read as the prop missing for reading, and are never written over: an
    edit on a day whose prop is odd does nothing (as `crewChange` in src/core/model/schedule.ts refuses).
  - `dayCrew` (src/core/model/schedule.ts) and the Shoot days tile's confirm list read the people on the day. Its
    comment and docs/SBD_FORMAT.md's line "Until call sheets (Phase 4) say who's called" change with it.
- **The call sheet shows only those on the day:** 4.1's Cast and Crew sections read the people on the day. Cast: those
  on the day who play a character in the day's scenes, as 4.1; a cast member on the day whose characters aren't in the
  day's scenes is still listed, under Cast, with no character. A character in the day's scenes whose actor isn't on
  the day shows "Not on the day" beside the actor's name (muted, with a second cue: the words themselves), so a missed
  actor is seen. Crew: those on the day by department, as 4.1.
- **Calls, on the `callsheet` record** (what the sheet adds; made on the first edit, as 4.1's notes):
  - `department_calls`: `{ "<department id>": "HH:MM" }`, a department's call where it differs from the day's crew
    call.
  - `person_times`: `{ "<person id>": { "call": "HH:MM", "hmu_ready": "HH:MM", "on_set": "HH:MM" } }`. `call` for
    anyone; `hmu_ready` and `on_set` are offered for cast only (someone in Cast on the sheet), but kept if present.
  - An empty field takes its key off (and an empty person entry is taken off). Odd values read as unset and are kept
    as they are when other keys are written (write back what this version doesn't understand, as `crewChange` does).
  - A person's call on the sheet: their own `call`; else the earliest override among their departments; else the
    day's crew call; else "—". The sheet shows just the time, not where it came from.
  - A department's heading row in Crew shows its call (the override, else the day's crew call), editable in place.
  - Each person row shows their call, editable in place. Cast rows show call, HMU ready and on set, each editable.
  - Time fields are edited the way the Shoot days tile edits call and wrap (read src/features/schedule/DaysTile.tsx and
    reuse its field, `isClockTime`); each is one edit when it's left.
- **Contacts:** a new prop on `person`, `contact_on_sheet`: true or false. Missing means the default: off for someone
  who's only cast (4.1's rule: departments exactly `['cast']`, or none and role `cast`), on for everyone else.
  - Cast and crew (src/features/people/PeopleTile.tsx, the person's editor, near phone and email): a switch, "Phone
    and email on call sheets", showing the default until set. One edit.
  - On the call sheet, a person's phone and email appear on their row only when their `contact_on_sheet` (or its
    default) is on, and only for people on the day. Never logged, never in History's words (History already keeps
    phone and email out; check src/features/history/summary.ts and keep it so).
  - "Only cast" is 4.1's `isCastOnly` in src/core/model/callsheet.ts; reuse it.
  - Document the prop in docs/SBD_FORMAT.md under "Person props (3.5)" (additive: no schema number, as `full_view`).
- **Where the edits live:** the "Who's on the day" list is a section of the call sheet, above Cast: the people on the
  day as chips or rows with a Take off button (labelled, keyboard reachable), an "Add person" Select of live people
  not on it, and "Add everyone". The Shoot days tile's confirm list shows only those on the day, and when none are,
  "Nobody on this day yet. Add people on its call sheet."
- **History:** the new edits read in History in en and es: "Added Ana Ruiz to Day 3", "Took Ana Ruiz off Day 3",
  "Added everyone to Day 3", "Camera's call on Day 3", "Ana Ruiz's call on Day 3" (the time itself may show as the
  field's before and after as other times do). Never the phone or email.
  - 4.1 marks every change to a `callsheet` record private in History (src/features/history/summary.ts, `fieldChanges`:
    the notes' text is never shown). Narrow that to the two note props (`notes`, `safety_notes`), so call times show
    their before and after; keep the notes private, and keep 4.1's tests for that passing.
