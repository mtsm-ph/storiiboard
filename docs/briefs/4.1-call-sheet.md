# Brief 4.1: the call sheet

For the builder (.claude/agents/builder.md). Written by the main session, Nov 10, 2026. Read CLAUDE.md first; its
Safety section comes before this brief. You start with no other context: everything you need is here or in the files
named.

## Goal

The Production workspace's Call sheet tile (tile id `sheet`, a placeholder today) shows a shoot day's call sheet, built
live from the project: pick a day and see what the crew would get. Nothing on it is copied from elsewhere (CLAUDE.md:
views read records, never copy data); the only things it stores are what a call sheet adds, its own notes, on a
`callsheet` record per day.

This is the first of Phase 4's call sheet milestones (docs/PLAN.md, "Phase 4 milestones"; docs/PHASE4_DECISIONS.md).
Later ones add who's on the day and their times (4.2), sun times (4.3), weather (4.4), the hospital (4.5), the PDF
(4.6) and issuing (4.7). Build 4.1 so each can add a section without reshaping it: one model function that returns the
sheet's data, and one small component per section.

## Decisions that apply

- **The day:** a Select at the top of the tile lists the project's live shoot days as the Shoot days tile labels them
  (`dayLabel` in src/core/model/shootDay.ts, "Day 3 · Mon, Nov 16"). It starts on the first dated day on or after
  today (this computer's local date), else the last day; the pick is the tile's own state, not stored. With no shoot
  days, the tile says so and points to the Shoot days tile (words only, no link needed). Without a project, the same
  "no project" line other Production tiles show.
- **What the sheet shows, top to bottom (each a section with a 10px uppercase mono label, as other tiles):**
  1. **The day:** the project's title (the project record's name, as the top bar shows it), the day's number "Day 3 of
     5" (its number among the live days, and how many there are), its date in full ("Monday, November 16, 2026" in the
     app's language, read at noon UTC as `dayDateLabel` does), crew call and wrap (the day's `call_time` and
     `wrap_time`, mono, "—" when unset). These are read only here; they're edited in the Shoot days tile.
  2. **Locations:** each live location the day's frames are shot at (`dayLocations` in src/core/model/schedule.ts), in
     that order: name, status (TBD, Confirmed, Cancelled, with its words from `LOCATION_STATUS_LABEL`), address,
     contact name and phone, parking, power. A field not filled in is left out (not printed empty), except the address,
     which reads "No address yet" in the warning colour (text-warning or the token Locations uses for a warning; check
     src/ui and design/DESIGN.md: Truffle, never orange). 4.8's checks will flag missing fields; don't add checks now.
  3. **Scenes:** the day's scenes in the order their frames are on the board (`dayScenes`): scene number (mono,
     `sceneLabel`), INT/EXT, the scene's name, DAY/NIGHT, pages (`pageLabel`), its location's name, how many of its
     frames are on this day ("3 frames", `tn`), and its characters (`sceneCharacters` in src/core/model/person.ts),
     each with who plays them (`castOf`), "Not cast" in muted text when nobody does.
  4. **Cast:** each person who plays a character in the day's scenes, once, with the characters they play on this day
     (in scene order), by the character's first appearance.
  5. **Crew:** everyone live in Cast and crew who isn't only cast, by department (`peopleByDepartment`, call-sheet
     order; someone in several departments is listed under each; those in none under "No department"). Each row: name,
     and title (or the role's words when there's no title). No phone or email yet: contacts come with 4.2, where the
     person's record says whether they print. Until 4.2 says who's on a day, a day's crew is everyone (as
     src/core/model/schedule.ts's `dayCrew` says today); write the model so 4.2 can pass in the people on the day.
     "Only cast" means the person's departments are exactly `['cast']`, or none and their role is the cast role (read
     `role: 'cast'` in src/core/model/role.ts). A cast-only person who plays no character in the day's scenes isn't
     on the sheet at all.
  6. **Notes:** two text areas of the call sheet's own: General notes and Safety notes. Each is one edit when it's
     left (blur), as other tiles' text areas are (GearTile, LocationsTile), so one ⌘Z.
- **The `callsheet` record:** type `callsheet` (already in `RECORD_TYPES`, src/core/model/record.ts), parent the
  `shoot_day`, so it goes with its day and a restored day brings it back. Props: `notes` and `safety_notes` (strings;
  an empty value unsets the prop, as `dayChange` does). It's made the first time a note is typed (the create and the
  set in the same op group, one undo), never just by viewing a day. If a day somehow has more than one live callsheet
  (two computers, a merged file), the first by `sortKey` is the call sheet and the others are ignored. No schema
  change and no migration: records are open JSON. Document the props in docs/SBD_FORMAT.md, a new section "Call sheet
  props (4.1)" written like "Shoot day props (3.7)" and "Gear props (3.9a)".
- **Trash:** a call sheet is never trashed on its own in 4.1 (no Delete button); it hides with its day. Check that the
  Trash tile (src/features/trash/trash.ts) and the views don't list a `callsheet` as an orphan when its day is in the
  Trash; if Trash would show it, leave it out the way other child records are handled there.
- **History:** a note edit reads in History like other edits, naming the item "Call sheet · Day 3" (or the day's
  label as History names days) and the field "General notes" or "Safety notes", in en and es. Read
  src/features/history/summary.ts: never the record type's raw name (its comment near `gear_item` says why). Creating
  the record with its first note reads as the note being set, not as "added a call sheet" plus a note, if summary.ts
  can do that the way it handles similar pairs; otherwise "Added the call sheet for Day 3" is fine.
- **Look:** a call sheet on paper, inside the tile (design/DESIGN.md, design/tokens; build from src/ui and the semantic
  token classes, no hex, no `--sb-*` names, no radius, no shadow). It scrolls inside the tile. Technical values
  (scene numbers, times, pages) in JetBrains Mono. Department names may carry their tag colours as other tiles do,
  never as state.
- **Tile words:** `tile.sheet.title` stays "Call sheet"; `tile.sheet.note` is no longer shown once the tile is built
  (check src/app/shell/tileContent.tsx: registering the tile there is what builds it). Every new word in
  src/core/i18n/locales/en.ts and es.ts.
- **The tile's model function:** in a new src/core/model/callsheet.ts, a pure function such as
  `callSheet(records, projectId, dayId, people?)` returning the sections' data (day, locations, scenes with characters
  and cast, cast, crew groups, notes), plus `dayCallsheet(records, dayId)` for the record. The tile only renders it.
  The edits (`callsheetNoteChange`) in src/features/callsheets/callsheetEdits.ts, returning `Change[]` as
  src/features/schedule/dayEdits.ts does. Trashed records are skipped everywhere (frames, scenes, locations, people,
  characters).
