# Brief 6.2b: doors and windows, shared rooms, copies

Phase 6, second half of milestone 6.2 (docs/PLAN.md; docs/PHASE6_DECISIONS.md decision 2). Read CLAUDE.md first
(its "Bird's-eye view" rules), then decision 2, then docs/briefs/6.2a-plan-real-units.md (what 6.2a built: the plan
host in src/features/birdseye/plan, src/core/model/plan.ts, lengths, walls as rotated rectangles) and
docs/briefs/canvas-core-map.md. Stay on the branch checked out.

## Goal

Doors and windows sit in walls. A room is drawn once and shared: a scene starts from a new room or one already
drawn, and can take its own copy. A new setup can start from another setup's state. An item can be moved to "this
setup only".

## Decisions that apply (Kevin, Nov 13, decision 2)

- The room (walls, doors, windows, set pieces) is its own record that scenes point to (`scene.room_id` → a `plan`
  of kind `room` under the project). A set that appears in three scenes is one room: a moved wall moves in all of
  them. Shown live, never copied, unless the person asks for a copy.
- Blocking belongs to the scene (`plan` kind `blocking`); cameras, lights and the rest to the setup (`plan` kind
  `setup`). A new setup starts empty or from another setup's state, copied.
- Anything can also be placed in one setup only (a cheated mark, an extra set piece): it then sits in the setup's
  plan instead of the room's or the scene's.
- No new table, record type or schema version. New props are added to docs/SBD_FORMAT.md's schema 14 section.

## What to build

1. **Doors and windows** (`plan_item` kinds `door`, `window`): each belongs to a wall (`wall_id`), at a distance
   along it from the wall's start (`along_m`, to the opening's centre), with a width (`width_m`; door 0.9 m, window
   1.2 m by default). They have no x, y or rotation of their own: their place is worked out from the wall, so they
   move, turn and stretch with it. A door also has `hinge` (`start` or `end`: which end of the opening the hinge is
   on) and `swing` (`left` or `right` of the wall's direction): drawn as the usual plan symbol, a gap in the wall, the
   leaf and its quarter-circle arc; a window as a gap with a thin double line. Add `hinge` and `swing` to
   SBD_FORMAT.md.
   - Tools Door (key D) and Window (key N, or the next free letter; say which): hover a wall and the opening
     previews where it would sit, snapped to the grid step along the wall; click places it. Not over a wall: nothing.
   - Drag slides it along its wall (never off the ends, never overlapping another opening); dragged onto another
     wall it moves to that wall (`wall_id` changes) in the same op. Arrow keys nudge it along the wall.
   - The Selection tile: width, distance from each end of the wall (typed lengths; editing one moves it), hinge and
     swing for a door (also F flips the swing, Shift+F the hinge), `height_m` when set.
   - A wall made shorter than its openings need: refuse the resize at the limit. Trashing a wall trashes its
     openings in the same op group and restoring it brings them back (as board items and their connectors do).
   - The canvas core picks by rotated box: give an opening a box (its width by the wall's thickness plus the swing
     for hit and culling purposes) through the existing item geometry; if the core needs an item whose geometry is
     derived, add the smallest option for it and say what you added.
2. **Starting a scene's room** replaces 6.2a's single "Start the room" button with a small chooser, in words:
   "New room", and "Use a room already drawn" listing the project's live rooms: first those whose `location_id` is
   this scene's location, then the rest, each with its name, its location and how many scenes use it. Choosing one
   sets the scene's `room_id` (one op). A new room takes the scene's location as its `location_id` when it has one.
3. **The room's menu** in the Plan tile's header (a Menu from src/ui): Rename; "Use another room…" (the chooser
   again; the scene's old room is left as it is, never trashed by this); "Make a separate copy" (only offered when
   another scene also uses this room): copies the room plan and every live item in it (openings re-pointed to the
   copied walls) in one op group and points this scene at the copy, named "<name> (SC 04)"; and "Delete room"
   (only when no other live scene uses it: trashes the plan and its items; the scene reads as having no room).
   The header says, in muted words, when a room is shared: "Also in SC 02, SC 07".
4. **A setup's state from another setup**: when a setup has no setup-level items, the Plan tile offers, quietly,
   "Start from another setup" with a choice of the scene's other setups that have items; it copies that setup's
   plan items into this setup's plan in one op group (creating the plan record if needed). Nothing links the two
   afterwards. (No setup-level items can be made until 6.3: build and test the copy on records made in the test.)
5. **This setup only**: for a selected room-level or scene-level item while a setup is shown, the Selection tile
   has a switch "This setup only". On: the item is moved (a `move` op) into the setup's plan, so other setups and
   scenes no longer show it. Off: it moves back to the level its kind belongs to (the room for walls, openings and
   set pieces; the scene's blocking for marks and paths). A wall moves with its openings. The item list a setup
   shows already orders room, blocking, setup (6.2a); an item made setup-only keeps its place in that order.
6. History lines (en and es) for each new action; the Trash lists openings under their room; export round trip.
