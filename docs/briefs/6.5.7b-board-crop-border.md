## What to build

1. **Crop, stored on the item.** A picture `board_item` gains an optional `crop` prop: the kept part as
   fractions of the picture, `{ x, y, width, height }` each 0..1 (x + width ≤ 1, y + height ≤ 1; absent means
   the whole picture). Listed in the 6.5 schema version's section under "Added to this version by later 6.5
   milestones". The stored picture, its previews and the recorded original are never changed or re-encoded.
   - **The gesture:** Ctrl (Cmd on a Mac) held when the drag of a handle STARTS makes that drag a crop: the
     dragged edge or corner moves in over the picture, the opposite side stays where it is on the board, and the
     picture's pixels stay the same size on the board (the item's box shrinks; the part outside is cut). Dragging
     outward again (still a crop drag) reveals what was cut, up to the picture's own edge and no further. While
     cropping, the part being cut shows dimmed so the person sees what is left. A minimum of 8 screen pixels a
     side. Releasing writes one op group (the item's geometry and `crop` together), so one undo restores both.
     Shift during a crop drag keeps the crop's proportions; Escape cancels. Without the key a handle resizes
     exactly as now, and a cropped picture resizes as a whole (its crop fractions unchanged, its box scaling).
   - A turned picture crops along its own axes. A locked item doesn't crop. Several items selected: the key
     does nothing special (they resize as now).
   - **Keyboard way:** in the picture's fields (where its size is edited), four number fields "Crop: left, top,
     right, bottom" in percent, and a **"Reset crop"** button (also in the item's context menu), which restores
     the whole picture in one op, the item's box growing back so that the part that was visible stays exactly
     where it is on the board (the uncropped picture's box is worked out from the crop).
     Reset is itself undoable.
   - **Drawing:** the canvas draws only the kept part, from the same preview sizes as now (the preview choice is
     by the picture's size on screen, uncropped). Fit-to-page, alignment, snapping, connectors and selection
     outlines use the cropped box.
   - **Export:** the board's PDF and PNG print the kept part only, at the same quality rule as now (the full
     picture's resolution, the original where full-quality export uses it), clipped, never re-encoded smaller
     than the uncropped export would have made that region. Copy/paste and Duplicate keep the crop. A picture
     replaced with another keeps its box and drops the crop (say so in a comment) or keeps it; choose and say.
   - Show in Finder / Explorer and the original pointer are untouched.
2. **A border on a picture.** Optional props on the picture item: `border` (on/off), `border_color` (any colour:
   the six swatches and the colour control text has; missing means ink, so in the Dark view a default border
   draws light, a chosen one doesn't change), `border_width` (in board units, 1 to 40, default 2; a number field
   and a small stepper). Drawn on the edge of the (cropped) box, inside it, so the item's size doesn't change;
   it turns and scales with the item; printed in PDF and PNG exactly. Fields beside the crop fields; each change
   an op. Listed in SBD_FORMAT.md with `crop`.
3. Both features on boards and decks. Video cards and shot/scene cards are not pictures: unchanged.
