## Kevin's words

"Trackpad mode: a toggle in Settings and in the canvas's zoom control, on by default on a Mac and off on Windows.
- On: two fingers pan and a pinch zooms, as now.
- Off: the wheel zooms at the pointer; Ctrl + wheel scrolls up and down, Shift + wheel sideways.
- A pinch zooms in both."

## What to build

1. **The setting** `trackpadMode: boolean | null` in settings.ts: null (never chosen) reads as on on a Mac and off
   elsewhere (use the platform reader the app already has; in the browser dev build, the same reader), so a
   person who never touched it gets the platform's default and a person who chose keeps their choice. One reader
   function, `trackpadModeOf(settings, platform)`. This computer's, never the project's; never in a preset.
2. **The toggles**: Settings → App, a switch "Trackpad mode" with one line of help ("Two fingers pan. Off: the
   wheel zooms."), and the same switch in the canvas's zoom control (its menu or cluster) on plans and boards.
   Both show and change the one setting, at once. en and es through t().
3. **Behaviour**, in the canvas core, for every host (plans, boards, decks; NOT the location map, the Player or the
   storyboard, which keep what they do):
   - On: exactly today's behaviour (wheel deltas pan in both axes; a pinch zooms at the pointer). No change, and
     the existing tests of it pass untouched.
   - Off: a plain wheel zooms at the pointer (the point under the pointer stays put), one notch a sensible step
     (the zoom control's own step, or a factor per 100 px of delta; say which), clamped to the canvas's zoom
     limits; Ctrl + wheel scrolls the view up and down; Shift + wheel scrolls it sideways (some systems already
     turn Shift + wheel into deltaX: handle both so it never scrolls twice or diagonally).
   - A pinch zooms in both modes. On a trackpad in Chromium and WebKit a pinch arrives as a wheel event with
     ctrlKey set and small non-integer deltas; a real Ctrl + mouse wheel arrives with ctrlKey and line-sized
     deltas (deltaMode or |deltaY| of a whole notch, typically 100 or 120 per notch; WebKit on a Mac also sends
     gesture events). In Off mode the two must be told apart: say exactly how you do it, test both, and prefer
     the gesture events on WebKit where they exist. If they cannot be told apart reliably on some engine, stop and
     report what you measured instead of guessing: Kevin decides.
   - The page itself never zooms or scrolls from the canvas (preventDefault as today), and Ctrl/Cmd + wheel
     never triggers the webview's own zoom.
4. Nothing else about tools or keys changes. Held Space, H and the hand tool pan in both modes.

## Tests

- Vitest: `trackpadModeOf` (null on a Mac, null elsewhere, true, false, odd stored values read as null); the pure
  function that turns a wheel event's fields and the mode into an action (pan dx/dy, zoom factor at point,
  scroll), table-driven: plain wheel, Ctrl + notch wheel, Shift + wheel (deltaY and deltaX forms), pinch
  (ctrlKey, fractional deltas), in both modes.
- e2e (new file e2e/trackpadMode.spec.ts, Chromium): with the mode off, a wheel over a plan zooms and the world
  point under the pointer stays within 1 px; Ctrl + wheel moves the view up/down only; Shift + wheel sideways
  only; a synthetic pinch (wheel with ctrlKey and fractional delta) zooms in both modes; with the mode on, a
  wheel pans as before. The switch in Settings and the one in the zoom control stay in step, and the choice

- e2e/macWebKit.spec.ts or wherever the Mac suite's canvas wheel tests live: add (don't change) one WebKit test of
  the off-mode zoom and the pinch, so the Mac run covers it.
