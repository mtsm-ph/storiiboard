# Brief 6.6.1 (build mode 1): the room tool; push and pull a wall (Kevin, Nov 16)

## Four rules first (Kevin, Nov 14). They come before everything else in this brief.

1. Logs and probes (throwaway tests and scripts, command output you keep, screenshots) go in test-results/, never
   the repo root or e2e/. Playwright empties test-results/e2e as a run starts, so use another folder under
   test-results/ (here: test-results/build661/).
2. Run only tools already in the repo's dependencies (package.json and Cargo.toml): nothing fetched through npx.
   `npx <tool>` only for a tool node_modules already holds (playwright, vitest, tsc, eslint). Edit files with the
   Edit tool ONLY (no shell appends or heredocs, no sed, no python). If the work needs another tool, stop and report.
3. Never weaken, skip or delete an existing test or assertion to get your work through: no `.skip`, `.fixme` or
   `.only`, no looser matcher, tolerance or timeout, no assertion removed or commented out. If an existing test
   fails and this brief didn't ask for it to change, stop and report the test's name, what it expects and what it
   got. Only a brief can say an existing test changes, and it names the test. The budgets of e2e/perf.spec.ts
   never move.
4. The reviewer treats any changed or removed assertion in an existing test as a finding unless this brief asked
   for it, so list in your report every existing test you changed, with the old and new lines.

## What to build

1. **The room tool.** A new tool in the plan's strip, after Wall: "Room" (its own SVG in toolIcons/, drawn in
   the kit's style: a square of four walls; one file, listed in the README), with a key that is free on the plan
   today (check keys.ts and the plan's own keys; proposed: Shift+W; say which you took and why), shown in its
   tooltip.
   - Press, drag, release: a rectangle follows the pointer (drawn live as four walls at the wall tool's
     thickness, with its width and depth shown in the plan's units as the wall run shows a length); release makes
     FOUR walls in ONE op group, one undo, at the level the wall tool writes to today (the room, or this setup
     only when that mode is on). The walls meet at their middle lines' ends exactly (the corners are shared
     points), as a closed wall run does. Corners snap as a wall's ends snap today (to other walls' ends, to the
     plan's snap points); Shift makes it square; a drag smaller than the wall tool's least length makes nothing.
     Escape cancels.
   - Typed sizes: while dragging, or right after release while the room is still selected, typing "4 x 3", "4m x
     3m", "12ft x 10ft" or 12' x 10' and Enter sets the room's inner or middle-line size (say which, and be
     consistent with what the wall run's typed length means), through length.ts.
   - Keyboard: with the tool chosen and the canvas focused, Enter places a default room (4 × 3 m) at the middle
     of what is seen, as other tools' keyboard placing does; then the typed size applies.
   - After placing: the four walls are selected and the tool stays Room or returns to Select, whichever the wall
     tool does today (consistent).
   - A deferred write carries the project it began in and writes nothing into another (CLAUDE.md, 6.4).
2. **Push and pull a wall.** With Select: dragging a wall by its BODY, perpendicular to itself, when its ends
   meet other walls' ends (shared corners, within the snap tolerance), moves that wall and stretches or shortens
   the walls that meet its ends so the corners stay joined: the room grows or shrinks on that side. In one op
   group on release, one undo.
   - Only the component of the drag across the wall counts when the wall is joined at both ends (it can't slide
     along itself and tear the corners); a wall joined at one end pivots nothing: it moves, and the one joined
     neighbour follows at that end; a wall joined at neither end moves freely exactly as today.
   - Neighbours: any number of walls sharing that corner follow (a T junction: a wall that ENDS on this wall's
     middle is not a corner: it keeps its place and length; say what you do and test it).
   - Doors and windows in the moved wall move with it; those in a stretched neighbour keep their place measured
     from the END THAT DIDN'T MOVE (openings.ts already keeps openings on a resized wall: use it), and one that
     would fall off a shortened wall stops the shrink at its edge (the room can't be pulled through its own
     door): the drag clamps there.
   - A neighbour can't shrink below the least wall length: the drag clamps.
   - Turned rooms: all of it works for walls at any angle (the drag's component along the wall's normal).
   - Alt held while dragging moves the wall alone, as today (free move), if Alt is free for that on the plan;
     else say which key, or none, and how a person detaches a wall.
   - The live cut of cones and beams follows during the drag as it does for a moved wall today (liveSights), for
     the moved wall AND the stretched neighbours; the drop writes what was shown.
   - Several walls selected: they move together as today (no push-pull) unless they are exactly one wall; say so.
   - Locked layers, a hidden art layer, a wall in another level (a setup-only wall joined to a room wall): a wall
     the person can't edit in this view doesn't follow; the drag then moves the wall alone or is refused: choose
     the safe one and say.
3. Nothing stored is new: walls are walls. No schema change.
