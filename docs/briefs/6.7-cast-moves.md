# Brief 6.7: cast marks and moves

Phase 6, milestone 6.7 (docs/PLAN.md; docs/PHASE6_DECISIONS.md decisions 2 and 9). Read CLAUDE.md first (its
"Bird's-eye view" rules), then those decisions, then the briefs of what is built: docs/briefs/6.2b (levels, "This
setup only"), 6.3 (icons, the Library, vector drawing), 6.4 (cameras, `beyond` drawings, upright labels, per-records
memoised readers), 6.5 (the legend). Stay on the branch checked out.

## Goal

Cast blocking on the plan: a mark per character, with numbered positions and the path between them, at the
scene's level so every setup of the scene shows it. Camera moves the same way: a camera's later positions with the
path between them, at the setup's level. Track and sliders show their real length.

## Decisions that apply (decision 9, with Kevin's level rule from decision 2)

- **A mark** is a `plan_item` of `kind: 'mark'`. It belongs either to a character (`character_id`: one of the
  scene's characters; find how a scene holds its characters, 3.x and schema 12's `character_ids`, and read
  through that) or to a camera (`camera_id`: a new prop, the id of a `camera` item in the same setup's plan: the
  camera's later positions). A mark with neither is a plain mark (a T-mark on the floor). Document `camera_id` in
  docs/SBD_FORMAT.md's schema 14 section and name it in the report.
- **Level**: a character's marks and plain marks sit in the scene's BLOCKING plan (`plan` kind `blocking` under the
  scene, made on demand in the same op group), so each of the scene's setups shows them; "This setup only" (6.2b)
  moves one into the setup's plan (a cheated mark). A camera's marks sit in the SETUP's plan with their camera.
- **Positions are numbered, never stored**: a character's marks in a view are ordered by plan order (sort key,
  blocking and setup-only together as the view shows them) and numbered 1, 2, 3. A camera's own item is its
  position 1 and its marks are 2, 3, … One reader gives, for a view: each character's ordered positions, each
  camera's ordered positions. Stale links read as none: a `character_id` that is trashed, gone or no longer in the
  scene's characters makes the mark a plain mark (kept, and the Selection tile says "This character is no longer
  in the scene"); a `camera_id` naming a trashed, missing or other-setup camera makes it a plain mark too.
- **Paths are worked out, never stored**: between consecutive positions of one character or one camera a
  straight dashed line with an arrowhead at the later end, drawn by the plan (kit's walk-path look: ink, dashed;
  never themed). A camera's path shows its total length in the unit shown at its last position ("3.20 m move").
  No `path` items are written in this milestone; leave the `path` kind and `points` in the format doc as they are.
- **What a mark looks like**: a character's first position uses the kit's `cast/cast-mark` drawing, later ones
  `cast/next-position`, a plain mark `cast/tape-mark`, each at its real size (below), with an upright mono label:
  the character's name (as the app shows character names) and the position number ("ANA 1"). A camera's later
  position draws the camera's own icon dimmed (the core's `dim`) at the mark's place and rotation (where it points
  there), with the camera's letter and the number ("A 2"), and thin cone edges only (no fill) for the same lens.
- Default sizes, metres, in iconSizes.ts: cast-mark 0.5 × 0.5; next-position 0.5 × 0.5; tape-mark 0.3 × 0.3;
  eyeline 1.5 × 0.2; background 0.5 × 0.5 (one extra; a group is several). `cast/eyeline` and `cast/background`
  are plain `icon` items at the blocking level (6.3's level rule for the cast group already says so) and become
  placeable; `cast/walk-path` is a picture of a path, not an item (muted in the Library with its own note, as
  the beam picture is).
- **Track and sliders**: a camera placed as `camera/dolly-track`, `camera/slider` or `camera/jib-crane` shows its
  length (its longer side) as an upright mono label in the unit shown ("3.00 m track"), and resizes along its
  length without keeping proportions (the other icons keep theirs).
- The legend (6.5) gains a cast section: each character with how many positions it has; and camera lines say
  "moves 3.20 m" when the camera has later positions.
- Copies: a setup copied from another copies the camera's marks re-pointed to the copied camera (`camera_id`
  through the id map) or, when their camera isn't in the copy, as plain marks; a room copy doesn't touch blocking;
  paste and duplicate of a mark keep its character, and keep its camera only when the camera is pasted with it
  (else plain).
- Trashing a camera trashes its marks in the same op group and restoring brings them back (as a wall and its
  openings). Trashing a character (in Cast and crew) leaves its marks, which then read as plain marks.
- No new dependency, table, record type or schema version.

## What to build

1. **Model** (src/core/model/planBlocking.ts + test): mark fields; the readers above with a per-records memo
   (the pattern in planCamera.ts); the edits as op lists: place a mark (plain, for a character, the next
   position for a character or a camera), give a mark its character, move a position earlier or later (a reorder
   among that owner's positions only), unlink.
2. **Tools and Library**: a Mark tool (key M if free on the plan; say which): click places a plain mark; with a
   mark or camera selected, the "Next position" action (button in the Selection tile, the `cast/next-position`
   entry in the Library, and the key N only if free: else Shift+M) places the next position 1 m to the right of
   the last one, selected, so arrow keys and drag place it. The Library's cast group is placeable as described.
3. **Drawing** in the plan host: marks, labels, the worked-out paths (as one `beyond`-style drawing per owner so
   culling keeps a path whose ends are off screen: the path belongs to the first position's item, with bounds
   covering every position), ghost cameras with cone edges, length labels. Memoised so a path is rebuilt when any
   of its positions, the character's name, the camera's lens look, the unit or the language change.
4. **The Selection tile**: for a mark: who it is (a choice of the scene's characters, the setup's cameras, or
   "Plain mark"), its position ("2 of 3") with Earlier and Later buttons, "Next position", "This setup only" (for
   blocking-level marks), rotation (for a camera's), X and Y. For a camera with later positions: the move's length
   and a list of its positions.
5. History lines (en and es): mark placed (naming the character or camera), given to a character, moved earlier
   or later, next position added, deleted, restored. Trash, export round trip, the legend.
6. Every write path covered by `unreadableAfter` and `WRITTEN_KINDS` (planItems.ts), a `madeIn` guard on anything
   deferred.
