# Brief 5.0: the canvas core, and boards

For the builder (.claude/agents/builder.md). Written by the main session, Nov 10, 2026. Read CLAUDE.md first; its
Safety section comes before this brief. You start with no other context: everything you need is here or in the files
named.

## Goal

The first Phase 5 milestone (docs/PLAN.md, "Phase 5 milestones", 5.0; Kevin's answers in docs/PHASE5_DECISIONS.md):
a react-konva canvas core that moodboards and decks use now and Phase 6's bird's-eye plans use later, and the boards
it draws. A person opens the Moodboard section, opens a board, draws rectangles (the one item kind in 5.0; 5.1 adds
text, stickies, shapes in any colour, pictures, video cards and links), and pans, zooms, selects, moves, resizes,
rotates, reorders, copies, pastes and deletes them, every change an op that ⌘Z undoes.

## Decisions that apply

- **Records** (decision 1; `board` and `board_item` are already in RECORD_TYPES, src/core/model/record.ts, so no
  migration and no schema_version change; document the props in docs/SBD_FORMAT.md, a new "Boards (5.0)" section and a
  Change log line):
  - `board` under the project: `kind: 'moodboard'` (5.5 adds `'deck'`), `department: Department | null`, `name:
    string | null`. A department's board has `name: null` and shows its department's translated label (stored values
    never change with the language); a free board has a name.
  - `board_item` under its board: `kind: 'rect'`, `x`, `y`, `width`, `height` (world units), `rotation` (degrees,
    clockwise, around the item's centre). **Z order is sibling order** (the records' `sortKey`, src/core/ops/order.ts:
    ordered children, as CLAUDE.md's data model says): bring to front is a `move` to the last key. Say in SBD_FORMAT.md
    that this is decision 1's "z order".
  - A rectangle in 5.0 has no colour prop: it draws in the theme's raised surface with an ink 1.5px outline. 5.1 adds
    colour.
- **Boards** (decision 2): many per project. A department's board is created the first time it's opened, never
  ahead, so a new project has none.
  - The Moodboard tile (tile id `moodboard`, already in src/app/shell/workspaces.ts) with no board open shows the board
    picker: the boards that exist, then the departments (src/core/model/department.ts, call-sheet order) whose board
    doesn't exist yet, then "New board" (asks a name). Opening a department's missing board creates it (one op group)
    and opens it. A department whose board is in the Trash: opening it restores that board (a restore op), never makes
    a second one. At most one board per department that isn't trashed.
  - The sidebar's Moodboard section lists the existing boards inline, as the Storyboard section lists its scenes
    (src/app/shell/sidebar/Sidebar.tsx, SceneList.tsx, navKeys.ts: same keys, same look), with "+ Add Board" opening
    the picker. Delete on a board's row moves it to the Trash, with its items.
  - The tile's title bar names the open board and has a way back to the picker; a free board's name is editable there
    (src/ui/EditableTitle.tsx).
  - The Moodboard workspace is no longer "coming": its `phase` becomes null (workspaces.ts), and the tile's note text
    goes. **This changes the shell screenshots** (the section loses "Phase 5" and its muted look): don't regenerate
    any baseline; the main session does. Report which visual tests fail and why.
- **The core** (decision 11), in `src/ui/canvas/`, knowing nothing about boards: a `Canvas` component taking items
  (id, x, y, width, height, rotation, and a draw function or kind), the selection, and callbacks that report finished
  gestures in world units. src/features/moodboards/ turns board_item records into items and gestures into `Change`s
  through `projectStore.apply` (src/app/projectStore.ts, src/core/ops/ops.ts). Pure, tested modules for the math:
  viewport (screen↔world, zoom around a point, clamp, fit to bounds), hit tests (a point and a marquee against rotated
  rectangles), z order, nudges, paste offsets.
  - World units are abstract: nothing in the core assumes pixels, so Phase 6 can give a scale in metres. Don't build
    the units option, only keep it possible.
  - Colours come from the theme's tokens read with getComputedStyle, as src/features/locations/LocationMap.tsx does
    (`themeColours`), and follow theme changes (its MutationObserver). Design (design/DESIGN.md, CLAUDE.md, Design): no
    radius, no shadow; the selection and handles are ink; orange only where the rules allow, never on the canvas.
  - Draw only the items whose bounds meet the view (5.7 does the rest of the performance work).
- **Viewport:** zoom 10% to 800%. Kept per board for the session, in memory: never an op, never in the project.
  - Pan: Space + drag, middle-button drag, the Hand tool (H), or a two-finger scroll (a wheel event without Ctrl/⌘).
  - Zoom: Ctrl/⌘ + wheel around the pointer; a trackpad pinch (Chromium sends it as Ctrl + wheel; WebKit sends
    `gesturestart`/`gesturechange`: handle both, WebKit is the Mac app's engine); `+` and `-` with the canvas focused;
    Shift+0 to 100%, Shift+1 to fit everything, Shift+2 to fit the selection.
  - A minimap, bottom right over the canvas, toggled by a labelled button: every item as a box and the view as a
    rectangle; click or drag in it moves the view.
- **Tools:** Select (V), Hand (H), Rectangle (R), as buttons with labels in a strip at the top of the tile (src/ui
  components; mono uppercase labels). Rectangle: drag to draw, or click to place 200 × 120; from the keyboard, Enter
  places one at the view's centre. After drawing, the tool goes back to Select and the new item is selected.
- **Selection:** click; Shift+click adds or removes; a marquee dragged on empty space selects every item it touches;
  ⌘A selects all; Escape clears. Selection is view state, never an op.
  - Move by drag; arrows nudge 1 unit, Shift+arrows 10 (with nothing selected, arrows pan).
  - Resize and rotate with Konva's Transformer: square ink handles, a rotate handle; Shift keeps the proportions while
    resizing and snaps the rotation to 15°. Smallest size 8 × 8.
  - Z order: ⌘] forward, ⌘[ backward, ⌘⇧] to front, ⌘⇧[ to back (Ctrl on Windows), also in a context menu
    (right-click, Shift+F10 or the menu key; src/ui/Menu.tsx) with Copy, Cut, Paste, Duplicate and Delete.
  - **One gesture is one op group:** a drag, a resize, a rotate or a reorder of several items writes one group, on
    release; a nudge is one group per key press.
- **Copy and paste:** ⌘C, ⌘X, ⌘V, ⌘D (duplicate). A clipboard inside the app (in memory, across boards and projects
  open in this window); nothing goes to the system clipboard in 5.0 (5.1 reads pictures from it). Paste puts the items
  at the pointer when it's over the canvas, else offset 16 units from the originals; pasted items are selected.
- **Delete:** Delete or Backspace moves the selected items to the Trash (a trash op: trashed_at, trashed_by; undo
  brings them back). The Trash (src/features/trash/trash.ts, TrashTile.tsx) gets two kinds: a board (its name or
  department) and a board item ("Rectangle on Camera board"), filtered and restored like the others. A trashed board's
  items go with it and come back with it. Views skip trashed records.
- **Undo:** ⌘Z and ⌘⇧Z work on the canvas as everywhere (src/app/shell/undoShortcuts.ts); a selection whose items an
  undo removed loses them.
- **History and notifications** name board and item changes in words (src/features/history, notifications: find how
  other records are named and add these), every word through t()/tn() in en and es.
- **Accessibility:** everything works from the keyboard. The canvas's focusable container has a label (the board's
  name) and a live region that says what's selected ("2 items selected", "Rectangle selected"). With the canvas
  focused and something selected, Tab and Shift+Tab select the next and previous item in z order; with nothing
  selected, Tab leaves the canvas. Every button has a label; focus rings are ink. The Moodboard workspace joins the
  axe sweep (e2e/a11ySweep.spec.ts), light and dark.
- **Lossless JSON export** (Phase 2, docs/SBD_FORMAT.md "Project data as JSON") round-trips boards and items: a test.

## Tests to add

- Vitest: viewport math (zoom around a point keeps it fixed, the 10%–800% clamp, fit to bounds and to an empty board);
  hit tests on rotated rectangles (a point; a marquee touching, missing, inside); z order (forward, backward, front,
  back, several items, sortKeys); a drag of three items is one op group, undo puts all three back; resize and rotate
  changes; nudge; paste offset and paste at a point; department board made once (a second open doesn't add one; one in
  the Trash is restored, not duplicated); Trash listing and restore of a board with its items, and of one item; History
  words; the JSON round trip.
- Playwright (Chromium, `?files=memory`): open Moodboard, open the Camera board from the picker (created), draw
  rectangles, click and marquee selection, drag to move, resize and rotate by handles, ⌘⇧] to front, copy and paste,
  delete then restore from the Trash, ⌘Z and ⌘⇧Z on each, reload and the board is as left; a keyboard-only pass (Tab
  in, R, Enter, arrows, Tab between items, Delete); the minimap moves the view; the sidebar lists the board and its
  Delete trashes it. No console errors.
