Read CLAUDE.md first (its "Bird's-eye view" rules), then that part of the decisions, then the briefs of what is

## Goal

Two kit icons go live. North: one per room; turning it sets the room's north. Sun path: on a setup's plan, the
real sun for the place and date the setup's frames are shot, with the hours marked and the sun's direction and
height at a time set on the item.

## Decisions that apply (Kevin, Nov 13)

- **North** is a `plan_item` of `kind: 'north'` in the ROOM's plan (icon `production/north`), so every scene and
  setup using the room shares it. **One per room**: placing it when the room has one selects the existing one and
  says so; a copy of a room copies it; paste never makes a second. The room's north IS that item's `rotation`:
  0 means north is up the plan, 90 means north points right (clockwise, as every rotation). No separate prop. A room
  with no north item has no north. Turning the item with its handle or the turn keys, or typing "North: 35°" in
  the Selection tile, sets it. It can be moved anywhere; only its rotation matters. Default size 0.6 × 0.6 m.
- **The sun path** is a `plan_item` of `kind: 'sun'` in the SETUP's plan (icon `lighting/sun-path`), with `time`
  (a time of day "HH:mm", in the location's time zone; default 12:00) and geometry (its position is the centre of
  the drawing; default size 0.8 × 0.8 m; its own rotation is ignored and its rotate handle hidden if the core
  allows, else rotation is simply not used).
- **What it reads, live, never copied**: the setup's frames (6.0's reader); among them the frames that have both a
  shoot day with a date (`shoot_day_id`) and a location with coordinates (the frame's own location when it has
  one, else its scene's: there is one reader for a frame's location already: find and use it). It uses the frame
  with the earliest shoot day (then board order). When the setup's frames are on several days or at several
  places, the Selection tile says which one is drawn ("Day 2 · Tue, Nov 17 at Diner; this setup also shoots on Day
  4") and nothing else changes.
- **With no location, day or north it says what's missing and draws nothing** (Kevin): the item shows its icon
  and a short mono label naming exactly what is missing, each through `t()`: "No frames in this setup", "No shoot
  day", "The shoot day has no date", "No location", "The location has no place on the map" (no coordinates), "No
  north in this room", "No time zone" (when neither the location's `time_zone` nor the lookup gives one: the lookup
  is a Rust call that may be unavailable in the browser build: handle both).
- **What it draws** when it has everything, around its centre, in metres on the plan, never themed:
  - The arc of the sun's direction from sunrise to sunset on a circle of radius 3 m (a constant; no new prop):
    for each time, the compass bearing of the sun (0° = north, clockwise) turned by the room's north onto the
    plan. Marks on the arc at every whole hour with the hour in mono ("7", "8", … in 24-hour form; 12-hour where
    the app already formats times that way: follow the call sheet's time format). Sunrise and sunset marked with
    their times. The golden-hour parts of the arc (sun between 6° above and 4° below the horizon, as CLAUDE.md
    defines for call sheets) in the golden hour colour (the fixed Burning Flame token, never themed: use the token
    constant, no hex in the component); the rest ink.
  - The sun at the item's `time`: a line from the centre toward the sun's direction to the arc, a small sun mark
    there, and a mono label: the time, the bearing and the height, "14:30 · 212° · 38° high"; below the horizon:
    "21:10 · the sun is down". A second thin line pointing the opposite way labelled "shadows" (where shadows
    fall), since that is what a gaffer reads.
  - It reaches outside the item's box: use `beyond` as the camera cone does, with bounds that cover the 3 m circle
    and labels.
- **The maths**, src/core/model/sunPath.ts (+ test), pure: from latitude, longitude, a date, a time zone and a
  time of day, the sun's compass bearing (degrees from north, clockwise) and altitude (degrees), through SunCalc
  (`getPosition`: its azimuth is measured from south, positive toward west, in radians: bearing = azimuth in
  degrees + 180), and the list of hour marks between sunrise and sunset for that date in that zone (DST-safe: build
  each instant from the zone, never from this computer's zone). Reuse sunTimes.ts for rise, set and golden hour.
- **Tests against published values, never a live service** (US Naval Observatory, aa.usno.navy.mil, fetched
  Nov 14, 2026; put the source in a comment). New York City, 40.7128, −74.0060, 21 June 2026, time zone
  America/New_York (UTC−4 that day):
  - 09:00: altitude 37.79°, azimuth 90.19°.
  - 13:00: altitude 72.72°, azimuth 181.63°.
  - 17:00: altitude 36.99°, azimuth 270.50°.
  (USNO's are geometric altitudes, without refraction: compare SunCalc's unrefracted altitude; tolerance 0.3° for
  altitude and 0.5° for azimuth.)
  - Sunrise 05:25, upper transit 12:58, sunset 20:31 (tolerance 2 minutes for rise and set).
  - New York, 21 December 2026 (UTC−5): sunrise 07:17, sunset 16:32.
  - Los Angeles, 34.0522, −118.2437, 20 March 2026 (UTC−7): sunrise 06:56, sunset 19:05.
  - Sydney, −33.8688, 151.2093, 21 June 2026 (UTC+10): sunrise 07:00, sunset 16:54 (southern hemisphere: the sun
    crosses the north; assert the noon bearing is within 5° of 0/360).
  And on the plan: with north turned 90°, a sun bearing 180° draws pointing left (west of the plan's up is... work
  it out from the definition above and assert the vector, with a comment showing the working).
- No new dependency (SunCalc is in). No network: this draws from maths and from the location's stored
  coordinates and zone. No new table, record type or schema version; `time` is in schema 14's list. Any other prop
  is added to docs/SBD_FORMAT.md and named in the report.

## What to build

1. The model (sunPath.ts; readers in src/core/model for a room's north and a setup's sun source with its
   "what's missing" result).
2. Library: `production/north` and `lighting/sun-path` placeable; sizes in iconSizes.ts.
3. Drawing in the plan host (north: the kit icon turned; sun: as above), memoised so it is rebuilt when any
   input changes: the item, the room's north item, the setup's frames, their day's date, the location's
   coordinates or zone, the unit, the language.
4. The Selection tile: north: "North" as a typed angle, with the note that every scene using the room shares it;
   sun: the time (a time field, keyboard steps of 15 minutes with arrow keys, Shift 1 hour), what it is drawn
   for (day, date, place, zone), the numbers at that time (bearing, height, sunrise, sunset, golden hour times as
   the call sheet gives them), or what is missing with a button that goes to where it is fixed (the frame's shoot
   day in Shoot days; the location in Locations; "Place north" selecting the north icon in the Library).
5. History lines (en and es): north placed, turned ("Turned the room's north to 35°"), sun path placed, its time
   set. Trash; export round trip. A room copy copies north; a setup copy copies the sun item with its time.
