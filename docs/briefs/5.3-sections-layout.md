## Decisions that apply

- **Sections** (a `board_item` of kind `section`; props in docs/SBD_FORMAT.md's Boards section and Change log; no
  migration, no schema_version change): geometry like other items (no rotation: a section is always upright), `name`
  (may be empty: shows "Section"), `color` from a fixed set of six light tints (board content colours, constants with
  the others in items.ts, each passing AA for ink text), default the first.
  - **What a section holds is worked out, not stored:** the items (and other sections) whose bounding box lies wholly
    inside it when a gesture starts. Items keep the board as their parent; no record changes when something is put in
    or taken out of a section. Say this in SBD_FORMAT.md.
  - Moving a section moves what it holds, in the same op group (connectors follow their items as in 5.2; a connector's
    free end inside the section moves too). Resizing a section moves nothing else.
  - Sections draw under connectors and items: the order is sections (in their sibling order), then connectors, then
    items. Z-order commands on a section reorder it among sections only.
  - The name is drawn above the section's top-left corner, in Inter Tight, and is part of its hit area; double-click
    or Enter on a selected section edits the name in place (5.1a's editor). Clicking inside a section on empty space
    selects the section only when the click is on its name or its edge band (8 screen px); elsewhere a click starts a
    marquee, so items inside stay easy to select.
  - Section tool (Shift+S, since S is the sticky): drag to draw one; drawn around a selection with ⌘⌥G (Ctrl+Alt+G on
    Windows) ("Section around selection": the selection's bounds plus 40 units of padding and room for the name), one
    op group.
  - Delete trashes the section only; its contents stay. The context menu has "Select contents".
  - Copy/paste and duplicate of a section alone copy only the section; with its contents selected they come too.
- **Align and distribute** (pure functions in src/ui/canvas/, board-agnostic): on two or more selected items (sections
  count as items; connectors are left out, they follow), using each item's axis-aligned bounding box (rotated items
  included): align left, centre, right, top, middle, bottom to the selection's bounds; distribute horizontally or
  vertically (three or more: equal gaps between neighbours, the outermost stay put). Keys: Alt+A, Alt+H, Alt+D, Alt+W,
  Alt+V, Alt+S for left, centre, right, top, middle, bottom; Alt+Shift+H and Alt+Shift+V distribute (Option on
  macOS: read `event.code`, since Option changes `event.key`). In the context menu and an "Arrange" group in the
  properties strip. One op group each.
- **Tidy up** (pure, board-agnostic): on two or more selected items, arrange them as a row, a column or a grid
  (columns = ceil(√n)), in reading order (by top, then left, with rows grouped by overlap), 24 units apart, keeping the
  selection's top-left corner. Grid cells take the widest item's width and the tallest's height; items sit at the top
  left of their cell. One op group. In the context menu and the Arrange group; Alt+Shift+T tidies as a grid.
- **Snap guides** while moving items (and while drawing or resizing a section or a shape, if it fits the same code):
  the moving selection's left, centre and right and its top, middle and bottom snap to the same lines of items in view
  within 6 screen px; the guides draw as 1px ink dashed lines across the items they align to, only while the gesture
  lasts. Holding Ctrl (⌘ on macOS) while dragging turns snapping off. Items being moved never snap to themselves or to
  what they carry (a section's contents). The snap search runs over items in view only and stays fast with 500 items
  (a timing test, as 5.2 has for routing).
- Every word through t()/tn() in en and es, History names the new steps ("Aligned 3 items", "Tidied 6 items into a
  grid", "Added a section"); the canvas's help text lists the new keys; the Trash lists a section ("Section 'Act One'
  on Camera board").
- Accessibility: Arrange commands are buttons with labels; the live region says what was done ("Aligned 3 items
  left"); a section is announced with its name and how many items it holds.

## Tests to add

- Vitest: each align and both distributes, with rotated items and mixed sizes; tidy row, column and grid (reading
  order, gaps, cell sizes, the top-left kept); snapping (each line kind, the threshold scales with zoom, no
  self-snapping, Ctrl turns it off) and a 500-item timing guard; section contents (wholly inside only, nested
  sections, connectors' free ends); moving a section moves its contents in one group and undo puts all back; resizing
  moves nothing; "Section around selection"; delete trashes the section only; History and Trash words.
- Playwright (Chromium, `?files=memory`): draw a section, drop two stickies into it, move the section, the stickies
  move (read the hook); rename it in place; align three items left with Alt+A and distribute them; tidy six items into
  a grid; drag a sticky near another and see it snap (positions equal) and a guide appear (a hook), and not snap with
  Ctrl held; ⌘Z for each; keyboard-only: select items, Alt+Shift+T; axe light and dark with a section and guides.
  No console errors.
