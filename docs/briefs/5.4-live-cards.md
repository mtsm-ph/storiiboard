# Brief 5.4: live cards, palettes, the brand kit, and a board's links

For the builder (.claude/agents/builder.md). Written by the main session, Nov 11, 2026. Read CLAUDE.md first; its
Safety section comes before this brief. You start with no other context: everything you need is here or in the files
named.

## Goal

Milestone 5.4 (docs/PLAN.md, "Phase 5 milestones"; Kevin's answers in docs/PHASE5_DECISIONS.md, decision 6): shots
and scenes from the storyboard as live cards on a board that jump back to the storyboard; scene palette cards;
brand-kit cards from the project's company; and a board's links to scenes, characters and locations. Builds on 5.0 to
5.3: read docs/briefs/5.0-canvas-core.md, 5.1a, 5.1b, 5.2, 5.3-sections-layout.md, then src/ui/canvas/ and
src/features/moodboards/.

## Decisions that apply

- **Live, never copies** (decision 6; CLAUDE.md: views read records, they never copy data). A card stores only which
  record it shows; everything it draws is read from the project as it is now.
- **Shot cards:** `board_item` kind `shot_card` with `shot_id`. It draws the frame's picture (its thumbnail, through the
  same picture cache as 5.1b, the full picture up close), the shot number as on the slate (src/core/model/shotNumbers.ts:
  scene and shot numbers must match the slate exactly), its shot size, lens mm and movement in JetBrains Mono, and its
  status shape (Have ■, Need to shoot □, Source ◩: CLAUDE.md, Design). A frame with no picture shows its sketch if it
  has one (src/features/storyboard/sketch), else a plain card with the number.
- **Scene cards:** kind `scene_card` with `scene_id`: the heading as the board shows it ("SC 04 INT. KITCHEN – NIGHT"),
  how many frames, and the first four frames' thumbnails in a row.
- **Gone or trashed:** a card whose record is in the Trash draws dimmed with "In the Trash" and stays on the board; one
  whose record was deleted for good reads "Shot removed" or "Scene removed". Neither is an error.
- **Jump back:** double-click or Enter on a shot or scene card switches to the Storyboard workspace and selects that
  frame or scene (src/features/storyboard/selection.ts `selectShot`, the sidebar's scene selection), scrolled into
  view. A trashed one opens the Trash tile filtered to it instead.
- **Adding cards:** an "Add from storyboard" button in the tool strip opens a picker: scenes in order, each with its
  frames, searchable by scene number, heading or shot number; pick scenes or frames (several) and they land as a
  tidied row at the view's centre (5.3's tidy), in one op group. Also, in the Storyboard, a frame's and a scene's
  context menu gains "Add to board…" (choose a board, which opens with the cards placed and selected).
- **Scene palette cards:** kind `palette_card` with `scene_id`: the scene's palette as it is now (src/core/model/
  palette.ts `paletteOf` on the scene's props), swatches in order with their hex in JetBrains Mono, the scene's heading
  above. Click a swatch's hex to copy it (the app's Copy hex, src/features/storyboard/palette/actions.ts). A scene with
  no palette says so and offers to open it in the storyboard's palette editor.
- **Brand kit** (from Phase 3's company profiles, src/core/model/company.ts):
  - A company profile gains **brand colours** (up to eight `#RRGGBB`) and **brand fonts** (up to three family names),
    edited where profiles are edited today (Settings, the company profile form), and copied into the project's
    `company` copy on attach and on Update from profile, as its other details are. Additive props on the profile and the
    project record's `company` value; no migration; document them in docs/SBD_FORMAT.md ("Project record props") and
    its Change log. The fonts pick from 5.1a's font list (bundled and this computer's).
  - A `brand_card` kind: the company's name, logo (the project's logo picture), colours as swatches with hex, and fonts
    each set in its own face (5.1a's font loading and missing-font fallback). Live from the project's `company` copy.
    With no company attached it says so and links to Settings → company.
- **A board's links** (CLAUDE.md: "Moodboards … link to scenes, characters and locations"): the board record gains
  `scene_ids`, `character_ids` and `location_ids` (additive; documented). Shown in the board's strip as "Linked to:
  SC 04, MAYA, Rooftop" with an Edit button opening a picker of the three kinds. The links are also read the other way:
  the storyboard Inspector's scene section, the Locations tile's location, and the character in the Team/People
  views list the boards linked to them, each opening the board. Trashed boards aren't listed; a trashed linked record
  is dropped from the "Linked to" line.
- All cards are board items like any other: move, resize (they keep their aspect), z order, sections, connectors,
  align and tidy, copy/paste within the project (another project: left out with 5.1b's notice), Trash.
- History and the Trash name them ("Shot card 4A on Camera board"); the live region announces them ("Shot card 4A, MCU,
  50 mm"); every word through t()/tn(), en and es.

## Tests to add

- Vitest: a shot card reads the shot as it is now (change the lens on the frame, the card's data changes, no op on the
  card); slate numbers match the storyboard's; trashed and deleted-for-good states; a scene card's frame count and
  first four; the palette card reads the scene's palette; brand colours and fonts copied on attach and Update from
  profile, and limits (eight, three, hex only); the brand card reads the project's copy; a board's links: adding, a
  trashed record dropped from the line, the reverse lists; picker placement in one group; History and Trash words.
- Playwright (Chromium, `?files=memory`): add a frame and a scene from the picker; change the frame's shot size in the
  Inspector and the card shows it; double-click the card and the Storyboard shows that frame selected; trash the frame,
  the card says so; a palette card; a company with brand colours and a font attached, a brand card shows them; link a
  board to a scene and see it in the scene's Inspector; "Add to board…" from a frame; axe light and dark. No console
  errors.
