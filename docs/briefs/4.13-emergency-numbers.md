# Brief 4.13: ambulance numbers, and Latin America

For the builder (.claude/agents/builder.md). Written by the main session, Nov 10, 2026. Read CLAUDE.md first; its
Safety section comes before this brief.

## Goal (Kevin, Nov 10, answering #125)

Numbers that reach only an ambulance are labelled "Ambulance", not "Emergency"; the other Latin American countries
are added, each checked against an official government source (the main session checked them; the sources are below,
put each in a comment beside its entry in src/core/model/emergencyNumbers.ts).

## Decisions that apply

- The table's entries gain a kind per number: `emergency` (a general line: police, fire and ambulance) or `ambulance`
  (medical only), and a country can have one of each. Printed (safety box on screen and in the PDF, the location
  editor): "Emergency 911", "Ambulance 106", or both "Emergency 911 · Ambulance 141"; GB "Emergency 999 or 112" and IE
  "Emergency 112 or 999" as now. Words in en and es ("Emergencias", "Ambulancia").
- Relabel as ambulance (Kevin): JP 119, BR 192, CL 131, PE 106, AR 107.
- Add (main session's research, Nov 10, official sources):
  - Emergency 911: EC (ecu911.gob.ec, Decreto 988, the single number), CR (Ley 7566; mcj.go.cr's directory,
    poder-judicial.go.cr), HN (911.gob.hn, Sistema Nacional de Emergencias), PA (minsa.gob.pa: "el número nacional
    para emergencias es el 9-1-1"), DO (Ley 184-17, policianacional.gob.do; 911.gob.do), VE (ven911.gob.ve: the
    single line since 171 was replaced).
  - Emergency 123: CO (policia.gov.co, funcionpublica.gov.co: Línea única de emergencias nacional 123; CRT Resolución
    087 de 1997).
  - Emergency 911 and Ambulance 132: SV (presidencia.gob.sv and salud.gob.sv: Sistema de Emergencias Médicas 132).
  - Emergency 911 and Ambulance 105: UY (montevideo.gub.uy: Policía 911, Ambulancia MSP 105).
  - Emergency 911 and Ambulance 141: PY (mspbs.gov.py: "Llamá gratis al 141 ante urgencias en salud"; 911 Policía
    Nacional, Ley 4739).
  - Ambulance 122: GT (conred.gob.gt: Bomberos Voluntarios 122, the main ambulance service; there's no single
    number).
  - Ambulance 102: NI (minsa.gob.ni: Central de Ambulancias 102).
  - Ambulance 104: CU (sld.cu / gob.cu: SIUM 104).
  - Ambulance 116: HT (servicespublics.gouv.ht: Service ambulancier national 116).
  - BO stays out ("check locally"): 168 works in several departments (Santa Cruz, Chuquisaca, Oruro, Potosí, Tarija)
    but not nationwide (minsalud.gob.bo, 2025); the main session tells Kevin.
  - MX (911), PR (911) are already there.
- The fingerprint (recipe 1) hashes the country code, not the number: leave it alone.
