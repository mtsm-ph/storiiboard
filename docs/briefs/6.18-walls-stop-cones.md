# Brief 6.18: walls stop cones and beams

Read CLAUDE.md first (Safety before all; "Bird's-eye view" under Tool rules), docs/PHASE6_DECISIONS.md (2, 6, 8,
16), docs/SBD_FORMAT.md's plan sections, and docs/briefs/6.4-cameras.md, 6.5-lights.md, 6.2b-doors-rooms-copies.md
and 6.11-performance.md. A milestone of its own (Kevin, Nov 14).

## Kevin's words

"Walls stop cones and beams. A camera's cone and a light's beam end at the room's walls and pass through door and
window openings, on the plan and on paper. Each camera and light has a 'Stop at walls' switch, on by default, for a
wild wall. Set pieces never block."

## Decisions (the main session's, from those words; say in the report if the code makes one impossible)

1. **What blocks:** the walls of the room the plan shows (the room record's walls, and a wall placed in one setup
   only, if the model has those), each as the solid it is drawn as: its length and thickness. Nothing else: not
   set pieces, not flags or diffusion, not cast marks, not other cameras or lights, not the underlay.
2. **Openings pass:** a door or a window is a gap in its wall for its whole width, whether a door is drawn open or
   closed and whatever a window's height. A cone or beam goes through the gap and carries on to the next wall, or
   to its own drawn length if it meets none.
3. **What is cut:** a camera's field-of-view cone (6.4, src/features/birdseye/plan/cameraDraw.tsx and
   src/core/model/planCamera.ts) and a light's beam (6.5, lightDraw.tsx, planLight.ts). The cut shape is what a
   point at the cone's apex can see within the cone's angle and length: the visibility polygon of that wedge
   against the blocking walls. A camera or light standing inside a wall's solid, or outside the room, follows the
   same rule from where it is (outside looking in through a window: only the part through the window carries on).
   The sun's drawing (6.6), paths, labels, the measure tool and the legend are unchanged. The cone's and beam's
   edge lines follow the cut shape; where the shape ends at a wall it gets no extra line.
4. **The switch:** every camera and every light has **Stop at walls**, on by default, in its selection panel
   (CameraSelection.tsx, LightSelection.tsx) beside its other fields; off, its cone or beam draws as before this
   milestone (a wild wall: one that is flown out for the shot). Stored as the item's prop `stop_at_walls`, written
   only as `false`; absent means on, so older files open unchanged, cut, with **no schema version change and no
   migration** (decision 16: props only; a new prop is listed in SBD_FORMAT.md, which you update: the prop, its
   default, which items take it). Changing it is one op, undoable, shown in History in plain words ("Camera A
   stops at walls" / "…no longer stops at walls"; follow how History words other plan item changes), copied with
   the item by copy, paste and "new setup from another's state". A camera's move (6.7: positions with a path) uses
   the one switch for every position.
5. **Live:** moving a wall, resizing it, moving a door or window, moving or turning the camera or light, changing
   the lens or beam angle all redraw the cut at once, in every setup that shows the room (rooms are shared and
   live: decision 2).
6. **On paper:** the PDF and the PNG (src/features/export/planRender.tsx, planExport.ts, and 6.17's
   per-department prints if they are in this checkout) draw the same cut shapes from the same function as the
   canvas: one geometry module in src/core/model (suggest planSightlines.ts: pure, no Konva, no DOM, metres in,
   polygon out), used by both. In a department print that leaves the art department's items out, walls still
   print (they are the room) and still cut.
7. **Speed (decision 15):** the 300-item plan must still pan, zoom and drag at 60 fps. The cut for an item is
   worked out only when its inputs change (the item's place, turn, angle, length, switch, and the room's walls and
   openings), cached per item, never per frame; a wall edit recomputes only items whose wedge's bounding box
   meets that wall or that had it in their last result. Run e2e/perf.spec.ts as 6.11's brief says (SB_PERF=1) on
   the 300-item plan with at least 40 walls and report p95 before and after on this machine.
8. **Numbers:** work in metres with a small tolerance (1 mm) so a cone edge running along a wall face, a camera
   exactly on a wall's end, two walls meeting at a corner, and a door at the very end of a wall neither leak nor
   flicker. Corners where two walls' solids meet must not leak light between them.

## What to build

- The geometry module with unit tests (Vitest), each case with numbers worked by hand in a comment: an open room
  (no walls: the wedge unchanged, same points as today); a wall straight across (the wedge ends at its near face);
  a wall across half the wedge; a door in that wall (a strip through the gap, widening by similar triangles: check
  its width at a distance); a window likewise; two walls at a corner; the apex inside a wall; the apex outside
  the room; a wall behind the apex (no effect); a wedge wider than 180° if lights allow one; the tolerance cases
  of decision 8; `stop_at_walls: false` returns the uncut wedge.
- The canvas and print drawing switched to it; the switch in both selection panels (en and es through `t()`;
  Spanish is the builder's until Kevin's glossary pass: list the strings); the op, History's words, copy/paste;
  SBD_FORMAT.md.
- Tests: Vitest for the prop's reader (absent is on), History's words, copy keeps it; the written PDF's cone path
  ends at the wall (read the PDF as planPrint.test.ts does) and passes the door; end to end (a new spec or in the
  plan specs): draw a room, place a camera facing a wall, the cone's drawn shape ends at the wall (read the shape
  from the canvas's test hook as the other plan specs do, not from pixels); add a door, it passes; switch Stop at
  walls off, the full cone is back; undo restores; reload keeps it.
- Existing tests whose plans have both walls and cones or beams may now see a cut cone. Where an existing
  assertion checks a cone's or beam's full shape or length on a plan with walls in its way, **don't change the
  assertion**: stop and report the test's name and what it expects, and the main session decides (the usual fix
  is that the test's camera gets `stop_at_walls: false` in its setup, which is a change to its arrangement, not to
  what it asserts; do that only for tests the main session names).
- Screenshot baselines (e2e/visual.spec.ts) must not change; if a baselined screen shows a cone or beam near a
  wall, stop and report.

## What not to touch

Existing tests and assertions (see above); screenshot baselines; .github; .claude; src-tauri; the schema version.
No new dependency (no geometry library: the visibility polygon is a page of code).
