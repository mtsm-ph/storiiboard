# Brief 6.18, review fixes

Read docs/briefs/6.18-walls-stop-cones.md first: this continues it, same rules. The geometry, the prop and the
print path were reviewed as sound. **6.18 does not merge until 1 to 3 are done and the perf spec is measured on
Kevin's PC** (the cloud container was too slow and noisy to tell: pan at fit p95 16.8 ms before, 33 to 50 after).

1. **(High) Pan and marquee no longer hold 60 fps on the 300-item plan** (decisions 7 and 15). Nothing is
   recomputed per frame; the cost is how many corners Konva fills and strokes on every redraw:
   - src/core/model/planSightlines.ts near 285 adds a ray every 2° across a light's whole span even where every
     ray lands on one straight wall face; near 259 it adds three rays at every corner of every solid in reach,
     hidden ones included; nothing merges collinear neighbours. A cut all-round light comes to several hundred
     corners where it used to be two circles. Fix: drop every corner collinear (within the 1 mm tolerance) with
     its neighbours; add arc samples only where the ray reaches the far boundary (the beam's own length), never
     along a wall; skip corners that the nearer hit already hides.
   - src/features/birdseye/plan/planSights.ts near 148-161 and lightDraw.tsx near 79 draw a cut all-round light
     as 8 stacked full-size copies of that polygon (64 large fills a frame on the perf plan, and 8 visible steps
     where the uncut glow fades smoothly). On screen draw ONE closed `Line` with the same `fillRadialGradient*`
     the uncut Circle uses (lightDraw.tsx near 103-111); keep bands for print only if the PDF writer needs them
     (say whether it does).
   - Unit tests: a wedge against one long straight wall yields a polygon of a handful of corners (assert an upper
     bound); the all-round case likewise; the polygons' areas are unchanged within 1e-6 m² by the simplification
     (compare with the unsimplified result kept in the test).
2. **(Medium) Every polygon is written into the DOM on every edit, in production:** PlanTile.tsx near 624, 848,
   872 and 880 stringify all corners of every camera, ghost and light into `data-plan-data` whenever records
   change. Publish only what the tests read cheaply (`cut`, `empty`, a corner count and a bounding box per item),
   or put the full shapes behind the test hook the other plan specs use so production writes none. Update
   e2e/planSightlines.spec.ts's reads accordingly (a test this milestone added: say what changed).
3. **(Medium, decision 5: "at once") The cut is stale while an item or a wall is dragged or turned:** a camera
   dragged a metre toward a wall pokes its cone a metre through it until the drop; a wall dragged across a cone
   doesn't cut it until the drop. The canvas core already rebuilds moving items from its `draft` on every pointer
   move (src/ui/canvas/Canvas.tsx near 334-338), hands the host live geometry in `constrainMove` (near 770-773)
   and has the live turn in `onTransform` (near 649-665). Add the smallest hook that lets the plan host remake an
   item's beyond-drawing (its cone or beam) from live geometry during a drag or a turn: for the dragged cameras
   and lights themselves, and, when a wall or an opening is what moves, for the items whose wedge box meets it.
   One sightline is well under a frame; keep it to the items affected, and never write an op until the drop.
   Boards must be untouched (the hook is optional and unset there). Tests: Chromium, in
   e2e/planSightlines.spec.ts: mid-drag (mouse down, move, not yet up) the camera's published shape already ends
   at the wall it was dragged toward; mid-drag of a wall across a cone, the cone is already cut; Escape restores
   the shape from before.
4. **(Low) A light whose centre is inside a wall's solid** (a practical or sconce on the wall's line) loses its
   whole glow: look out from the face its wedge points to (or, for an all-round light, from both faces), instead
   of drawing nothing. A camera inside a wall still draws nothing.
5. **(Low) Hairline openings:** `END_HAIR_M` (planSightlines.ts near 23) is 1e-7 m while the drawing joins wall
   ends within 1 mm (wallShape.ts near 15), so two walls in line whose stored ends are 0.1 mm apart leave a slit;
   and each face is taken 1 mm in (near 114), so a T whose stem ends exactly on the other wall's face leaves a
   1 mm band open. Extend each solid's ends by the join tolerance instead.
6. **(Low) Tests the review found missing:** walls at arbitrary angles (30°, 45°, 73°) with hand-worked numbers;
   an apex 100 m from the origin; overlapping openings; the cut on a camera move's ghost positions; and
   `wallSolids` must clamp an opening that runs past the wall's end itself (near 334), not rely on its caller.
