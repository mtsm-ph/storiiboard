# Brief 6.5: lights, flags and diffusion

Phase 6, milestone 6.5 (docs/PLAN.md; docs/PHASE6_DECISIONS.md decisions 8 and 11). Read CLAUDE.md first (its
"Bird's-eye view" rules), then those decisions, then the briefs of what is built: docs/briefs/6.3-library.md (icon
items, the Library, iconSizes.ts, the drawing in iconDraw.tsx), 6.4-cameras.md (a camera's cone and the canvas
item's extra draw extent; the Frames tile), 6.2a and 6.2b (the plan host and levels). Stay on the branch checked
out.

## Goal

Lights with a beam, a height and watts; flags, bounces and diffusion at real sizes; each optionally the gear item
it is, so the day's gear check covers the plan. The setup's legend totals its watts.

## Decisions that apply (decision 8, with Kevin's additions, and 11)

- A light is a `plan_item` of `kind: 'light'` in the setup's plan, with `icon` (lighting/fresnel, led-panel, tube,
  cob-softbox, hmi-par, china-ball, practical, spacelight, rgb-gel, window-light), geometry as every item (its
  rotation is where it points), `name`, `height_m`, and:
  - `beam_deg`: its beam angle. Defaults by icon: fresnel 40, led-panel 100, tube 120, cob-softbox 90, hmi-par 30,
    rgb-gel 60, window-light 90; china-ball, practical and spacelight shine all round (360: drawn as a soft circle,
    no cone, and the field reads "All round").
  - `gear_id`: the `gear_item` it is, if the person picks one from the project's Gear (any item; lighting and
    grip-electric first).
  - `watts`: typed on the light. When absent and the light has a gear item with `watts`, that is its wattage, read
    live, never copied. Add a Watts field to Gear items in the Gear tile (`gear_item.watts`, documented in
    schema 14 already), any kind.
- Flags, bounces and diffusion are `plan_item`s of `kind: 'flag'` (grip/flag, floppy, neg-fill) and
  `kind: 'diffusion'` (grip/diffusion-4x4, bounce, overhead-8x8) in the setup's plan, with `icon`, `gear_id`,
  `name`, `height_m`. Seen from above a standing frame is a line: its `width` is the frame's width and its
  `height` (depth) 0.05 m; the overhead is a square. A "Size" choice in the Selection tile sets the real size by
  the names crews use, whatever unit the screen shows: 2×3, 4×4, 6×6, 8×8, 12×12, 20×20 (feet: 0.61 × 0.91 m,
  1.22, 1.83, 2.44, 3.66, 6.10 m square); typed sizes stay possible.
- Default sizes, metres (width × depth), added to iconSizes.ts: fresnel 0.4 × 0.4; led-panel 0.7 × 0.4; tube
  1.2 × 0.1; cob-softbox 0.9 × 0.9; hmi-par 0.5 × 0.6; china-ball 0.6 × 0.6; practical 0.3 × 0.3; spacelight
  0.8 × 0.8; rgb-gel 0.4 × 0.4; window-light 1.2 × 0.3; flag 0.91 × 0.05; floppy 1.22 × 0.05; neg-fill
  1.22 × 0.05; diffusion-4x4 1.22 × 0.05; bounce 1.22 × 0.05; overhead-8x8 2.44 × 2.44.
  `lighting/beam-cone` stays a picture of a beam (not an item); `lighting/sun-path` comes in 6.6.
- **The beam**: from the light's centre along its rotation, `beam_deg` wide, drawn 4 m long (a constant; no new
  prop), in the lighting department's fixed colour at low opacity with a thin edge of the same colour (department
  colours are fixed tokens that never change with the theme: find the token in design/tokens and src/ui and use
  it through a constant, not a hex in the component; plans are never themed). It uses the same extra draw extent
  cameras' cones use, so a beam stays drawn when its light's box is off screen.
- **Heights** (decision 11): any plan item with `height_m` set shows it as a small mono label by the item, in the
  unit shown (cameras from 6.4 already do: share that code).
- **The legend**, one function in src/core/model (planLegend.ts + test) that 6.10's export will print too: for a
  setup, its items by department (camera, lighting, grip, art, cast, sound, production), each line with its name
  or label, its count when repeated, its height when set, and for lights their watts; and the setup's total watts
  (a light with no wattage counts as unknown: the total reads "4,200 W + 2 unknown"). Shown in the Selection tile
  when nothing is selected (under the room's lines), mono for numbers.
- **The gear check** (src/core/checks/gearOnDay.ts and its test): a plan item with a `gear_id`, in a setup that
  has a frame in a shoot day, needs that gear on that day's list, exactly as a frame's `gear_ids` do; a missing one
  is the same finding, saying it comes from the plan ("on Setup 2's plan"). Trashed items, setups and gear don't
  count; the one readers apply.
- Copies (a setup started from another, paste, duplicate, the separate copy) keep `gear_id`, `watts`, `beam_deg`.
- No new dependency, table, record type or schema version. Any prop beyond the schema 14 list is added to
  docs/SBD_FORMAT.md and named in the report.

## What to build

1. Model: light, flag and diffusion fields and readers (a light's wattage through one reader), the legend, the
   size presets.
2. Library: these icons are placeable now (remove them from the not-yet list); L for the light tool only if free.
3. Drawing: icon, beam or all-round glow, height label; the rotate handle points the light.
4. The Selection tile: for a light: gear item, watts (typed, or "from the gear item: 1,200 W" read-only with a
   way to type over), beam angle or All round, height, name, rotation, X and Y; for a flag or diffusion: gear
   item, Size choice, width, height, name. Nothing selected: the legend.
5. Gear tile: Watts on a gear item.
6. The check, History lines (en and es), Trash, export round trip.
