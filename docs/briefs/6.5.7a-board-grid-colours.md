## What to build

1. **The dot grid** (boards and decks; not plans, which have their own scale).
   - Two settings on this computer (src/core/storage/settings.ts, read defensively): `boardGrid: boolean`
     (default off) and `boardGridSnap: boolean` (default off; it can only act while the grid is on). A view, as
     Paper/Dark is: never in the project, never an op, never printed or exported, not in thumbnails.
   - Switches in the board's zoom control cluster beside Paper/Dark: "Grid" and "Snap to grid" (the second
     disabled while the grid is off), accessible names, en and es.
   - **Known spacing that follows the zoom:** the base spacing is 20 board units. Dots are drawn at the spacing
     of the level that keeps them between about 12 and 60 screen pixels apart: 20 × 5^k board units for the
     integer k that fits (…, 4, 20, 100, 500, …), so every coarser level's dots are a subset of the finer one's
     and nothing jumps sideways when zooming. The current spacing is shown as a small mono label by the Grid
     switch ("20", "100"), so the spacing is known, not guessed.
   - Drawn behind every item, as dots about 1.5 screen px, in the view's soft ink (6.5.1's palette: readable but
     quiet on Paper and on Dark), from the visible area only: one cached pattern or one shape per frame, never a
     node per dot. Panning and zooming a 500-item board must measure as before (e2e/perf.spec.ts's board test).
   - **Snap** (when on): while dragging or resizing an item, its top-left corner (move) or the dragged edge or
     corner (resize) snaps to the nearest dot of the spacing currently shown, within the canvas core's existing
     snap distance; the existing snapping to other items' edges and centres keeps working and wins when both are
     in reach (say how ties are settled). Alt (Option) held suspends snapping if the core already has such a
     key; add none. Arrow-key nudges are unchanged. A deck's page frames snap like any item.
   - The snapped position is what is stored (ordinary geometry through the usual op); nothing else is stored.
2. **Any colour for notes and stickies.**
   - The note's and the sticky's colour control shows the six swatches as now, plus the same "any colour" control
     text items have (the Hex field and the picker; on a Mac the Hex field is the keyboard way, as Kevin decided
     Nov 15: no extra button). A chosen colour is stored where the swatch is stored today, as text stores its
     colour (say the prop and its form; if today's prop holds a swatch NAME, a custom colour is stored in a way
     that an older build reads as a missing or default colour rather than breaking, and it is listed in the 6.5
     schema version's section of docs/SBD_FORMAT.md under "Added to this version by later 6.5 milestones").
   - Text on a custom fill: ink or paper, whichever has the higher contrast against that fill (WCAG contrast
     ratio; one function, tested), unless the text has a colour of its own. This holds in Paper and Dark alike:
     a note's fill is a chosen colour and doesn't change with the view.
   - Recent custom colours, if the text control keeps them, are shared with it; add no new list otherwise.
   - PDF and PNG exports of a board print the custom fill and its text colour exactly (6.10/5.x paths).
