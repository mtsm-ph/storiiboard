# Brief 4.12: the company on the call sheet's header

For the builder (.claude/agents/builder.md). Written by the main session, Nov 10, 2026. Read CLAUDE.md first; its
Safety section comes before this brief.

## Goal (Kevin, Nov 10, answering #126)

The call sheet header prints the project's company name and logo when one is attached, with the project title; on
screen (the Call sheet tile's day section) and in the PDF (src/features/callsheets/callSheetPdf.ts).

## Decisions that apply

- The company: 3.2's company profiles (src/core/model/company.ts; Settings → Project attaches one; "the project
  keeps a copy of its details and logo"). Read how the project keeps the copy and its logo bytes (a media record or
  props: follow the code) and reuse it; nothing is fetched or copied anew.
- Header layout (PDF): the logo at the top left, at most 40 pt tall and 140 pt wide, aspect kept; the company name
  beside or under it in the label style; the project title as now, below. Without a company, the header is exactly as
  it is today (so sheets without a company print the same bytes as before for the same data).
- The issue stamp (4.7) keeps its top-right place; the logo never overlaps it (test both, en/Letter and es/A4, with a
  long company name and a wide logo).
- Logos: PNG and JPEG embed directly with pdf-lib; another format the project may hold (SVG, WebP): rasterize it in
  the page to PNG first if that's how the app already handles logos elsewhere (grep the storyboard/deck code), else
  print the name only and say so in the report.
- The fingerprint (recipe 1, frozen) doesn't include the company. Kevin's upgrade rule: an upgrade alone must never
  make an issued sheet read "Changed since issued". Leave the recipe alone; a company attached or changed later
  doesn't flag (note it for Kevin in the report).
- On screen: the company name and a small logo (24 px tall) above the project title in the day section.

## Tests

- Vitest: the PDF with a PNG logo and a JPEG logo (image drawn inside its box, name printed), without a company
  (identical text and layout to before), with a long name and the issue stamp (no overlap); the tile's day section
  renders the name.
- Playwright: attach a company with a logo in Settings → Project (read e2e/projects.spec.ts or the company tests for
  how), see it on the call sheet, export the PDF and find the company name in its text.
