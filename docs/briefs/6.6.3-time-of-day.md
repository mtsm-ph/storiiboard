## What to build

1. **A time of day on the sun path.** An optional prop on the sun path item: `time_minutes` (minutes after
   local midnight in the sun's zone, 0 to 1439; absent means "no time chosen": the path shows as today and
   position readers use solar noon as 6.5.5 does). An op when it changes.
2. **The slider.** In the sun path's settings (the Selection tile) and in the place the compass opens: a slider
   from sunrise to sunset of the sun's date (a native range input or the app's own slider component if one
   exists; keyboard: arrows 5 minutes, Page Up/Down 1 hour, Home sunrise, End sunset; its value text read out as
   "14:35, sun at 212°, 38° high"), with the time shown in JetBrains Mono, sunrise and sunset at its ends, marks
   for golden hour (6° above to 4° below the horizon, as call sheets define it) and solar noon, and a "Clear"
   that removes the time. Outside daylight the slider stops at its ends (night is not drawn).
   - **Drag and watch:** while dragging, the sun's marker on the path, the compass's sun, the windows' light
     patches (below) and the readout all follow LIVE, with no op per frame: one op on release (or when the
     keyboard changes it: one op per key press is fine; a held arrow key must not flood History: coalesce a
     burst into one group, or commit on key up; say which). Use the canvas core's live path (as liveSights does)
     or local state above the store; the plan must stay at one frame per move on the 300-item plan (measure it
     in a probe, not in perf.spec).
   - With no place, date or north, the slider is disabled and says what is missing (the reading rule's words).
   - Scout photos' "Show the sun at the photo's time" (6.27), if present on this branch: while that switch is
     on, the slider shows the photo's time and is read-only, saying so; switching it off gives the slider back.
3. **The marker.** At the chosen time the sun path draws the sun's position on its arc (a filled sun mark with
   the time beside it) and a short line from the plan's sun-path centre showing the light's direction (where
   shadows fall is the opposite way). The compass shows the same position. Printed as shown (the plan prints
   what it shows; the legend's sun line gains the time: "Sun: Nov 3, 14:35").
4. **Light patches on the floor ("if they fit": build them unless one of the stops below applies).** For each
   window in an outside wall that the sun is in front of at the chosen time (windowSun.ts), draw the patch of
   direct light on the floor: the window's glass (its width along the wall, from `sill_height_m` to
   `top_height_m`) projected along the sun's direction onto the floor: a parallelogram starting
   `sill / tan(altitude)` from the wall and ending `top / tan(altitude)` from it, sheared by the sun's bearing
   against the wall's normal, cut by the wall's thickness (start at the inner face).
   - Heights: where a window has no sill or top set, use defaults named in one place (sill 0.9 m, top 2.1 m)
     and say in the patch's hover/selection text "assuming a 0.9 m sill and a 2.1 m top"; a ceiling lower than
     the top caps it.
   - The patch is cut where it meets a wall (reuse planSightlines' cutting so a patch never crosses a wall; a
     door or window in that wall lets it through only if that is what the sightline code already does for
     beams: say what you did). Set pieces don't block it (unknown heights).
   - Drawn under items, above the underlay, as the golden-hour token's colour at a low opacity with a 1.5 px
     edge in the same colour, never themed by the app; in the Dark view it keeps its colour. A second cue beside
     colour: the patch carries the window's time label once ("14:35") if there is room.
   - Always with the line, wherever the patches are described (Selection tile, legend): "Buildings and trees
     outside aren't known."
   - Computed, never stored; cached per records object, view and time; recomputed live during the slider's drag
     at one frame per move (if it can't hold a frame with 10 windows, draw the patches on release only and say
     so).
   - Printed with the plan when the sun has a time, with the legend line "Direct light at 14:35".
   - **Stops** (then skip the patches, keep the slider and marker, and report why): the room's inside can't be
     told for most real plans; the cut against walls needs a rewrite of planSightlines; a frame can't be held
     and release-only looks wrong.
5. **For the MCP and 3D later:** the reading rule's result carries the chosen time and the sun's azimuth and
   altitude at it; `WindowSunOf` gains the patch polygon in metres at that time. Documented as what `get_plan`
   returns. No MCP code.
