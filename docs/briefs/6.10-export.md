# Brief 6.10: plans on paper (PDF and PNG; scene and shoot-day packets)

Phase 6, milestone 6.10 (docs/PLAN.md; docs/PHASE6_DECISIONS.md decision 14). This is what Kevin hands a gaffer
at the gate, so it must read on paper. Read CLAUDE.md first ("Export", "Call sheets" for paper by region, "Bird's-
eye view"), then decision 14, then how decks are exported (5.6a): src/features/export/deckRender.tsx (PageStage
from src/ui/canvas mounted off screen, `renderPage`), deckDisplay.ts (a Konva snapshot turned into display ops),
deckPdfWriter.ts (pdf-lib: text as text in its font, vector paths, pictures), deckPng.ts, and the export registry
(a format is one file plus one line). Then the plan host's drawing in src/features/birdseye/plan and the legend
(src/core/model/planLegend.ts). Stay on the branch checked out.

## Goal

A setup's plan as a PDF and as a PNG: to scale, with its scale and scale bar, a legend by department (heights,
watts, the setup's total), the setup's frames and lenses, and the scene heading. Two packets, each one PDF: a
scene (a page per setup) and a shoot day (every setup with a frame that day, in the day's order). Paper by region,
as call sheets.

## Decisions that apply

- **Vectors and real text, never a screenshot**: walls, openings, icons, cones, beams, the sun path, marks and
  paths, measures and labels are vector paths and text in their fonts (as the deck PDF does: text as text,
  fonts embedded where their `fsType` allows; the bundled faces always are). Only the underlay is a picture (from
  its original when it can be read, as deck pictures are, else the stored copy, saying so as the deck export does).
- **Never themed, and never this computer's view**: paper and ink whatever the theme; every layer is printed
  whatever the Layers tile says (6.8's state is a focus view); nothing selected, no previews, no handles; the
  underlay prints dimmed.
- **To scale, at a round scale**: the plan area of the page shows the setup's extent (the room's items, the
  scene's blocking, the setup's items, each item's fit extent, plus a margin) at the largest scale from this list
  that fits: metric (when the plan unit shown is metres) 1:20, 1:25, 1:50, 1:75, 1:100, 1:150, 1:200, 1:250,
  1:500, 1:1000; feet: 1" = 1', 3/4" = 1', 1/2" = 1', 3/8" = 1', 1/4" = 1', 3/16" = 1', 1/8" = 1', 1/16" = 1',
  1/32" = 1'. The page prints the scale in words ("Scale 1:50" or 1/4" = 1'-0") and a scale bar with a round real
  length; a test measures a wall on the page: a 4.00 m wall at 1:50 is 80.0 mm of PDF. (PageStage fits a "page"
  rectangle to its size: make that rectangle the plan area's paper size times the scale so the fit IS the scale.)
- **Paper by region** (Letter in the US and its neighbours, A4 elsewhere: reuse the call sheet's rule and its
  choice in the export dialog, 4.6), landscape. The PNG is the same page at 200 dpi.
- **The page** (design/DESIGN.md's feel: boxed, 1.5 px rules, mono labels; ink on paper; Inter Tight for words,
  JetBrains Mono for every technical value):
  - Header: the project's name (and the attached company's name as the call sheet header prints it, 4.12, no
    logo), the scene's slate and heading ("SC 04 · INT. DINER · DAY"), "Setup 2 · name", the shoot day when the
    setup's frames have one ("Day 2 · Tue, Nov 17"; several days: all, in order), the date printed, page n of m.
  - The plan area, boxed, with a light 1 m (or 1 ft) grid and the scale bar inside its bottom left corner.
  - A legend column on the right (or a band along the bottom when the plan is much wider than tall): from
    `setupLegend`: Room (north when set, wall heights), then each department's lines with counts, heights and
    watts, then "Total 4,200 W" (with unknowns as the legend says); then "Frames": per camera its letter and body
    ("A · Sony FX3, Full frame 16:9"), each frame's shot number, size, lens ("35 mm", "24–70 at 50 mm, 2×") and
    field of view ("51.1°"), and frames of the setup with no camera. Text that doesn't fit continues on a second
    page titled "… legend, continued", never shrunk below 7 pt and never cut.
  - A footer line: "Made with Storiiboard" is NOT printed; the footer is only the map-style credit lines that are
    owed: none here. (No footer.)
- **Line weights on paper**: the kit's 1.5 px lines print at 0.6 pt, hairlines (grid, cone edges) at 0.25 pt,
  walls are filled to scale; dashes scale with their line. Fills with opacity (cones, beams, all-round lights,
  the dimmed underlay, china-ball's light lines) print with real transparency.
- **The print path's known gaps, all to be closed here** (found in review; verify each before fixing):
  1. Icon text is a custom Konva `Shape` (fillText) the snapshot doesn't know: give it a print form (text in
     JetBrains Mono at its baseline).
  2. deckDisplay.ts `parsePathData` reads only M, L, C and Z and stops at the first A or Q: add arc and quadratic
     conversion to cubics there (tested against known circles: a circle of radius r made of two arcs has every
     point within 0.01 r of the true circle), so circles, rounded rects and the door's swing print whole.
  3. `parseColour` takes hex only: rgba paint is dropped. Carry opacity through to the PDF (an ExtGState with
     fill and stroke alpha) and to the PNG.
  4. Non-scaling strokes and dashes are screen pixels: in print they take the weights above (the draw context's
     `print` flag is already passed by PageStage: use it in the plan's drawings).
  5. `beyond` drawings (cones, beams, the sun path, paths, labels) aren't drawn by PageStage: draw them, beneath
     or above their item as on screen.
  6. Labels' paper halo (a paper-coloured stroke under the text) prints as a stroke under real text.
  Decks must come out exactly as before: every deck export test passes unchanged, and add a test that a deck page
  with an ellipse and a translucent shape prints the same bytes as before your change, or say precisely what
  differs and why it is an improvement that needs Kevin's eye.
- **Packets**: "Scene plans" is one PDF with a page per live setup of the scene in setup order (a scene with no
  setups: one page of its room and blocking). "Shoot day plans" is one PDF of every setup that has a Need-to-shoot
  frame in that day, ordered as the day shoots (the order the call sheet lists the day's scenes: find and reuse
  it; then setup number), each page's header naming the day. A setup with no room prints its items with a note
  "No room drawn". An empty packet isn't written: the export says there is nothing to print.
- **Where it is offered**: the export registry (src/features/export): "Setup plan (PDF)", "Setup plan (PNG)",
  "Scene plans (PDF)", "Shoot day plans (PDF)", so the Export Project window lists them for a scene or the whole
  project as it does other formats (whole project: every scene's packet in one PDF, in scene order); an "Export…"
  menu in the Plan tile's header for the shown setup and its scene; "Plans (PDF)" on a day in the Shoot days
  tile. Files go to disk through src/core/storage as every export (the desktop's save path allows .pdf and .png
  already: exportExtensions.test.ts must still pass). Export names follow the app's language as other exports' do
  ("<Project> SC004 Setup 2 plan.pdf" in the pattern existing exports use).
- No new dependency. No network. No schema change.

## What to build

1. src/features/export/planPages.ts (pure, + test): for a setup, the page's model: extent, the chosen scale,
   the plan area and legend boxes on the paper, the header's words, the legend's lines, continuation pages; for a
   scene and a day, the list of pages in order.
2. src/features/export/planRender.tsx: renders a page: the plan through PageStage with the plan host's own
   drawings (the same components the screen uses, so paper matches screen) in print mode, the header, legend,
   grid, scale bar and scale text as display ops; then the PDF through deckPdfWriter and the PNG at 200 dpi.
3. The six gaps above, each with its test, decks unchanged.
4. The registry entries, the Plan tile's Export menu, the Shoot days button, progress and errors as other
   exports show them, en and es for every word on the page and in the app.
5. A probe for the main session to look at (not committed): export a setup's PDF for a furnished room with a
   door, a window, a sofa, a camera with a linked frame, a fresnel with watts and height, a 4×4 flag, north and
   a sun path, and rasterise page 1 to test-results/probe-export/page1.png (pdf.js is already in the project for
   script import), A4 and Letter; and the scene packet's page count.
