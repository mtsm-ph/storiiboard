# Brief 5.5: decks: page frames, Present, and four templates

For the builder (.claude/agents/builder.md). Written by the main session, Nov 11, 2026. Read CLAUDE.md first; its
Safety section comes before this brief. You start with no other context: everything you need is here or in the files
named.

## Goal

Milestone 5.5 (docs/PLAN.md, "Phase 5 milestones"; Kevin's answers in docs/PHASE5_DECISIONS.md, decisions 3 and 8):
decks as boards of ordered page frames, presented page by page, and four film-native templates filled live from the
project. Builds on 5.0 to 5.4: read docs/briefs/5.0-canvas-core.md through 5.4-live-cards.md, then src/ui/canvas/ and
src/features/moodboards/. Export (PDF, PNG) is 5.6, not this milestone.

## Decisions that apply

- **A deck is a board** (decision 3): the `board` record's `kind` becomes `'deck'` (5.0 left room for it). The
  Moodboard section and picker list decks with boards, marked as decks; "New deck" in the picker asks a name, a
  template (Blank, Treatment, Lookbook, Pitch, Shot list) and, for Blank, a page format. Props documented in
  docs/SBD_FORMAT.md's Boards section and Change log; no migration, no schema_version change.
- **Page frames:** a `board_item` of kind `page` with `format` (`16:9`, `9:16`, `4:5`, `a4`, `letter`; decision 3:
  9:16 and 4:5 for social decks, Kevin Nov 10), `orientation` for A4 and Letter (`portrait` or `landscape`), `name`
  (may be empty), `background` (`#RRGGBB`, default `#FFFFFF`: board content, never themed).
  - World sizes: 16:9 is 1920 × 1080, 9:16 1080 × 1920, 4:5 1080 × 1350, A4 1240 × 1754, Letter 1275 × 1650 (portrait;
    swapped for landscape). Resizing keeps the format's ratio; pages never rotate.
  - **What a page holds is worked out, like a section's** (5.3): the items wholly inside it when a gesture starts.
    Moving a page carries them. Pages draw in the section layer, under connectors and items; a section inside a page
    draws above it (5.3's containment order). Items outside every page are working space and never print or present
    (decision 3).
  - **Page order is the pages' sibling order** (sortKey among pages only). A Pages panel (a column at the left of the
    deck's board view, collapsible) lists them in order with their number and name; drag to reorder, or Move up and
    Move down buttons (keyboard); Add page (the deck's format by default, placed 200 units right of the last page),
    Duplicate page (with its contents), Delete page (its contents stay as working space; one op group). Clicking a
    page in the panel centres the view on it. The canvas draws each page's number and name above its top-left corner.
  - Pages exist only on deck boards; the Page tool (P) and the panel appear only there.
- **Live field cards** (decision 6 again: decks fill from the project and stay in step with it). A `board_item` of kind
  `field_card` with `field` and text style props as 5.1a's text box has (font, weight, italic, size, colour, align):
  - `title` (the project's name), `logline`, `format`, `runtime`, `company` (the attached company's name), `dates` (the
    first and last shoot day, in the app's date format, or "Dates to come"), `cast` (each character and who plays
    them), `crew` (key crew by department, call-sheet order: name and role).
  - **Never contact details:** decks go to clients, so a people card shows names and roles only, never a phone,
    email or address (CLAUDE.md: contact details are private).
  - A field with nothing to show draws a muted placeholder on screen ("Logline") and nothing in a presentation.
  - The text box's editing doesn't apply (the words come from the project); double-click opens where the value is
    edited (Settings → Project for title, logline, format, runtime; the Team tile for cast and crew; Shoot days for
    dates).
- **Templates** (decision 8), each made in one op group as a new deck, its pages laid left to right 200 units apart:
  - **Treatment** (A4 or Letter by region, src/core/model/paper.ts `paperFor` with the app's paper setting; portrait):
    a title page (title, logline, company); format and runtime; characters (cast); then the scenes, four scene cards per
    page.
  - **Lookbook** (16:9): a title page; then one page per scene (at most 12, in order): its scene card, its palette card
    and up to four shot cards, tidied; a closing page with the brand-kit card.
  - **Pitch** (16:9): title; logline; the team (crew); the look (palette cards of the first four scenes with one);
    the schedule (dates); a closing page with the brand-kit card.
  - **Shot list** (A4 or Letter by region, landscape): per scene, a page with the scene card at the top and its shot
    cards in a 3 × 2 grid, more pages as needed.
  - Page headings ("Characters", "The look") are plain text boxes written in the app's language when the deck is made
    (authored content the person can change; stored text never changes with the language afterwards).
  - Empty projects still make a sensible deck (a title page and the placeholders).
- **Present** (decision 3): a Present button on a deck (and ⌘⌥P / Ctrl+Alt+P) opens an in-app presentation over the
  whole window: one page at a time, scaled to fit on black, the canvas's renderer read-only (live cards live, pictures
  at full size), the page number and count in a corner that fades. → / Space / Page Down next, ← / Page Up previous,
  Home and End, Escape leaves (focus returns to the Present button); F asks the webview for full screen through the
  Fullscreen API when it's available and does nothing otherwise. Starts at the selected page, else the first. Nothing
  outside pages is shown. Empty deck: Present is disabled with a reason.
- History and the Trash name pages ("Page 3 'The look' on Pitch deck"), field cards, decks; the live region announces
  the page while presenting ("Page 3 of 8, The look"); every word through t()/tn(), en and es.
