## What to build (on plans only; a board's tools are unchanged)

1. **The eyedropper.** A tool in the plan's strip, key E (confirm E is free on the plan; if something uses it,
   stop and report), its own SVG in toolIcons/ in the kit's style (a pipette), listed in the README.
   - Choose it, then click an item: that item becomes "what is being placed", and the tool changes to placing
     it: the pointer carries its outline (as a Library item being placed does), each click places a COPY there
     (centred on the pointer, snapping as that kind snaps), and it stays loaded for the next click until Escape,
     V, or another tool. Enter places one at the middle of what is seen (the keyboard way), as placing does today.
   - What a copy carries: everything Duplicate carries for that kind today (kind, icon, size, turn, colour,
     label style, a light's watts and beam, its gear link, a camera's body and lens choice if Duplicate keeps
     them: do exactly what Duplicate does, through the same function, so the two can't drift) and nothing
     Duplicate drops (a camera's frames, a mark's order: a copied mark is the next position as Duplicate makes
     it; say what Duplicate does for each kind and that the eyedropper matches).
   - What can be picked: any placed item of the Library's kinds (set pieces, cameras, lights, grip, sound,
     production items, marks) and a wall (then it loads the Wall tool with that wall's thickness, if walls carry
     one; else the Wall tool as it is), a door or a window (loads that opening's tool with its width and, since
     6.5.6, its sill and top or its door height). Things that can't be copied (north: one per room; the sun
     path if one per setup; the underlay): the pointer shows "not here" over them and a click does nothing; say
     which.
   - The copy goes where the ORIGINAL's kind goes today when placed new (its level: room, blocking, this setup),
     not where the original sits; a deferred write carries the project it began in (6.4).
   - Hidden or locked layers: an item you can't select can't be picked.
   - Alt + click with Select picks and loads in one go, if Alt + click is free on the plan (else skip and say).
2. **The delete tool.** A tool in the strip, after the eyedropper, its own SVG (an eraser or a crossed square in
   the kit's style: not a bin, which reads as the Trash tile), a free key (proposed: X; say which), in its
   tooltip.
   - Choose it, then click an item: it goes to the Trash (the same op as selecting it and pressing Delete: what
     goes with it goes with it: a wall's doors and windows, a camera's marks), one click one step, each undoable
     (⌘Z brings it back). The tool stays on for the next click until Escape or another tool.
   - Hover shows what a click will take: the item (and what goes with it) outlined in the warning colour's
     token with a second cue (a dashed outline), never colour alone.
   - Drag: nothing (no marquee delete): a click only, so nothing is taken by accident. A click on empty plan does
     nothing.
   - Locked or hidden layers: not deletable; the pointer shows "not here".
   - "Nothing you can break": after the first delete in a session of the tool, a small notice says once
     "Deleted items are in the Trash. ⌘Z brings the last one back." (through the app's notice, not a dialog).
3. Both tools: `aria-keyshortcuts`, names in en and es through t(), the strip's tooltip; keys never fire while
   typing. The strip's order on a plan: Select, Hand, Wall, Room, Door, Window, Camera, Light, Mark, Measure,
   Eyedropper, Delete (say if you place them elsewhere and why).
