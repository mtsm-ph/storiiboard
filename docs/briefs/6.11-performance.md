# Brief 6.11: performance (300 items at 60 fps)

Phase 6, milestone 6.11 (docs/PLAN.md; docs/PHASE6_DECISIONS.md decision 15: 300 items, pan, zoom and drag at
60 fps on Kevin's Mac and a mid Windows laptop, measured as 5.7's board was). Read CLAUDE.md first, then how 5.7
measured the board: e2e/perf.spec.ts (runs only with SB_PERF=1, Chromium, 1440 × 900: requestAnimationFrame gaps
during wheel pans, zooms, a drag and a marquee; reopen time; budgets), and what 5.7 did to the canvas core
(culling, memoised item nodes, zoom settle, bounded caches; docs/briefs/canvas-core-map.md section 8). Stay on the
branch checked out.

## Goal

A measured, repeatable test that a real plan of 300 items pans, zooms, drags and edits smoothly, and whatever
changes it takes to pass it without changing what a plan or a board looks like or does.

## The test (added to e2e/perf.spec.ts, same switch and style as the board's)

- Build, through the app's own edits in one or a few `applyWith` groups (as the board's test builds its items),
  one scene with a room and two setups, 300 items in the first setup's view: 40 walls forming rooms and a
  corridor (several with heights), 12 doors and windows, 80 set pieces and other icons (mixed, some turned), 40
  lights with beams (some all-round, some with gear and watts), 30 flags and diffusion frames, 6 cameras each
  linked to frames with lenses (cones drawn) and two with moves, 8 characters with 5 positions each (paths), 20
  measures, 12 production and sound items, north, a sun path with everything it needs (location with coordinates
  and zone, a dated shoot day), and one underlay picture (generated in the test as the board test generates its
  pictures). Assert the plan's data lists 300 items and that cones, beams, paths and the sun are drawn.
- Measure requestAnimationFrame gaps (median, p95, max) for: a wheel pan for 3 s at fit; the same at 400%; a
  zoom from fit to 400% and back; a drag of 20 selected items across 90 moves; a marquee over about 150 items;
  turning a light with the turn key held for 2 s; typing a wall's length in the Selection tile (10 edits: time from
  Enter to the next frame that shows the new value); placing 10 icons from the keyboard; and, with the plan open,
  20 lens edits to a linked frame in the Inspector (each must redraw that camera's cone: time per edit). Also the
  time to open the Setup section on that setup after a reload, and the JS heap after it.
- Budgets, as the board's test has them (they must hold on a CI runner; the report gives the real numbers):
  pan and zoom p95 under 34 ms; drag and marquee p95 under 50 ms; each edit under 100 ms; open under 3 s; no
  page errors. Decision 15's 60 fps is what the measured numbers on this computer should show (p95 near 16.7 ms
  for pan and zoom): if they don't, find why and fix it before reporting.

## Finding and fixing what is slow

Profile before changing anything (Chromium's tracing through Playwright, or `performance.measure` around the
plan's builders): the likely costs are full-record scans per edit in the plan's readers (they are memoised per
records object: check each is hit once per edit, not once per item), rebuilding canvas items whose inputs didn't
change (the memoised builder: assert object identity in a unit test for an unrelated edit and for an edit to one
item), drawings that reach beyond their box (cones, beams, the sun, paths, labels sized for the least zoom: are
they culled at all when off screen, and do labels cost a text measure per frame), the legend recomputed per
frame, and React re-rendering tiles that didn't change (Setups, Frames, Layers, Library, Selection) on every op.
Fix the top costs only, each with a unit test that pins the saving (a scan counter, an identity check); never by
dropping something from the drawing. Boards must be untouched: the board's own performance test still passes with
its numbers no worse (report them beside the earlier 5.7 numbers in docs/PLAN.md's 5.7 row).

## Not in this milestone

New features, export speed (report how long the 6.10 PDF of this plan takes, but only fix it if it is over
10 s), WebKit numbers (Kevin measures on his Mac by hand). Do not touch .github, .claude, src-tauri, package.json
or the kit's SVGs. Never regenerate baselines. No git commands that change anything. Logs and probes go to
test-results/; never overwrite an existing test file, add to it.
