# Phase 6 decisions (asked Nov 13, 2026; waiting for Kevin)

One page. Each decision: the question, what was weighed, the recommendation and why, and how hard it is to change
later. Nothing is built until Kevin answers. The plan is in `docs/PLAN.md`, "Phase 6 milestones"; the scope is
"Phase 6 notes" there (Kevin, Nov 3) and CLAUDE.md's bird's-eye rules.

| # | Question | Weighed | Recommended | Change later |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | How is a plan stored? | One blob per plan, or one `plan_item` record per item (the record type already exists). | **A record per item**, `plan_item` under its `plan`, as boards are (Phase 5, decision 1): every move an op, so undo, History, the Trash, suggestions and the MCP work unchanged. Positions and sizes in **metres**, always, whatever the screen shows. | Hard: it's the file format. |
| 2 | Does every setup redraw the room? | CLAUDE.md says one plan per scene setup. Taken literally, a scene with five setups has the walls drawn five times, and a moved wall is moved five times. | **One plan per setup, over a room drawn once.** The room (walls, doors, windows, set pieces) belongs to the scene and shows live in each of its setups' plans, never copied. What belongs to a setup is its state: cameras, lights, flags, diffusion, cast marks. "Copy room from another scene" covers two scenes in one location. | Hard once plans exist. |
| 3 | What is a setup, and how do frames join one? | Setups made by hand, or worked out from frames. | **A `setup` record under its scene** (the type exists), lettered in order, named by hand if wanted. A frame joins at most one (`setup_id`, which Shoot days' totals already read); frames with none stay setups of their own, as today. New setup from selected frames on the board, or in the Setup workspace. | Medium. |
| 4 | Where do camera bodies live? | A new record type, or the Gear catalogue. | **A camera body is a `gear_item`** with new props: sensor width and height in mm for the recording mode used (full frame, Super 35 crop, open gate), and a letter (A cam, B cam). A table in the code offers common bodies and modes with their published sensor sizes, each with its source, always editable by hand. The project's first body is the default for every camera on a plan. | Medium. |
| 5 | Zooms and anamorphic lenses | Props on each frame only, or lenses in the Gear catalogue. | **A lens is a `gear_item`** with focal range (prime: one number) and squeeze (1 by default; 1.33, 1.5, 1.8, 2). A frame may pick its lens (`lens_id`); `lens_mm` stays the focal length used and must sit in the range. Horizontal field of view = 2 × atan(sensor width × squeeze / (2 × focal length)). A frame with no lens picked works as today, squeeze 1. | Medium. |
| 6 | Which lens does a camera on the plan draw? | The camera keeps a lens of its own (two values that can disagree), or it reads its frames' lens live. | **Live from the frame** (CLAUDE.md: views never copy data). A camera is linked to the frames shot from it and draws the picked frame's field of view; change the lens on the board and the cone follows. A camera with no frame yet has a lens typed on it, and the check "storyboard lens differs from the plan's camera" fires only when a frame linked later disagrees. | Hard once plans rely on copies. |
| 7 | Metres or feet | A project setting, or this computer's. | **This computer's, by region** (feet and inches in the US, metres elsewhere), with a Settings override, as weather units are. The file is always metres. Lengths can be typed either way ("12ft", "3.5m"). Grid 0.5 m or 1 ft; a scale bar always on the plan. | Easy. |
| 8 | Lights, flags and diffusion | Free shapes, or a library of fixtures. | **A library from the 68 plan icons already in the kit**, by department, each with real default sizes. A light has a beam angle drawn as a cone, a name and, if wanted, the `gear_item` it is, so a light on the plan that the day's gear list lacks is found by the existing gear check. Flags and diffusion have real widths (4×4, 8×8, 12×12…). | Easy. |
| 9 | Cast marks and movement | Free arrows, or marks tied to characters. | **A mark per character** (the scene's `character` records), numbered positions 1, 2, 3 with a path between them, built on Phase 5's connectors. A camera move is the same: positions with a path (a dolly track has its real length). | Medium. |
| 10 | Department layers | Access control, or a focus view. | **A focus view, never access control** (as 3.6c). An item's layer comes from what it is: camera, lighting, grip, art, cast blocking. Each layer shows, hides, dims and locks; a person starts on their own department's layer with the others dimmed. | Easy. |
| 11 | Heights | 2D only, or keep heights for Phase 10's 3D. | **Keep them now, draw them later:** wall height, a camera's height (a frame's `height` from 3.18 when linked), a light's height, as plain fields in metres. Phase 6 draws nothing in 3D. | Easy now, a migration later. |
| 12 | A floor plan or location photo underneath | Draw every room from nothing, or trace. | **A picture underneath, set to scale by two points and a typed distance**, locked and dimmed; stored as board pictures are (2560 px with a pointer to the original). A measure tool reads any distance. | Easy. |
| 13 | Is a plan themed? | Recolour the icons with the theme (PLAN.md's Nov 2 note), or keep plans on paper as boards are. | **Never themed, as boards (Kevin, Nov 11):** the plan is paper with ink in light and dark, department colours fixed, so it prints and reads the same everywhere. Only the app around it follows the theme. | Easy. |
| 14 | Getting a plan out | Screen only, or files. | **PDF and PNG per setup** through the export registry: the plan to scale with a scale bar, a legend by department, the setup's frames and lenses, the scene heading. On the call sheet and the web: later (Phase 9 shows the latest plan read-only). | Easy. |
| 15 | How fast | No target, or a measured one. | **300 items, pan, zoom and drag at 60 fps** on Kevin's Mac and a mid Windows laptop, measured as 5.7's board was. | Easy. |
| 16 | The file format | New tables, or new props. | **No new table and no new record type:** `setup`, `plan` and `plan_item` exist; decisions 4, 5 and 11 add props to `gear_item`, frames and plan items. One schema version for the lot, documented in SBD_FORMAT.md, a backup before the upgrade, older files opening unchanged. **Needs Kevin's approval** (CLAUDE.md: ask before changing the schema). | Hard. |
