# Phase 4 decisions (Nov 9, 2026)

One page for Kevin. Each decision gives the question, the options, the recommendation and why, and how hard it is to
change later. The plan is in `docs/PLAN.md`, "Phase 4 milestones". Nothing in Phase 4 is built until Kevin answers.
Decisions 1, 2 and 4 change a CLAUDE.md network rule or add a dependency, so they need his answer in any case.

| # | Question | Options | Recommendation and why | Change later |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Where does the weather come from on a paid shoot? Open-Meteo's free API is for non-commercial use only ("You may only use the free API services for non-commercial purposes"), and CLAUDE.md names it. | (a) **MET Norway** Locationforecast 2.0 (api.met.no): free "for any purpose... This includes paid-for apps or services"; CC BY 4.0, credit "Data from MET Norway"; global (ECMWF's model; the Nordic countries get extra local work); about 9 days ahead; asks for an identifying User-Agent, coordinates cut to 4 decimals, and no repeat before the `Expires` time (outside the Nordics it updates every 6 hours). (b) **US National Weather Service** (api.weather.gov): "free to use for any purpose", public domain, but US only and 7 days ahead. (c) **Open-Meteo's paid plan**: a commercial licence and a key for the publisher (Standard, 1M calls a month; no price on its page), not one each user brings. (d) Keep Open-Meteo free: not allowed on paid shoots. | **(a) MET Norway everywhere**: free for paid work, one host on net.rs's list, worldwide (Kevin shoots in the US and Latin America), and its terms fit how the app asks (only when a day's call sheet is opened or refreshed, cached in `lookups` with when it was fetched). (b) can be added later for US severe-weather alerts. CLAUDE.md's weather rule changes from Open-Meteo to MET Norway, which needs Kevin's yes. | Easy: one source behind one module |
| 2 | How is the nearest hospital found? | (a) **Nominatim**, already on the host list for the address search: "hospital" searched in a box round the location, only when the person presses Find, 1 request a second, cached; the app sorts what comes back by straight-line distance. (b) **Overpass** (OSM's query service): exact (`amenity=hospital`, with `emergency=yes` where mapped), but its public server says "Commercial use should use self-hosted or paid Overpass servers", and lists "relying on the public instances as backend" for an app as a problem. (c) **Google Places** with the person's own key from Settings: the best data, but needs a key. (d) Typed by hand only. | **(a), with (d) always open, and (c) when a Google key is set.** Press Find nearest hospital on a location: up to five hospitals with their distance (as the crow flies, said so) and emergency department where OSM knows it; the person picks one and it's kept on the location (name, address, phone, coordinates), editable by hand, so every call sheet at that place has it. Driving time needs a routing service: not in Phase 4. The call sheet credits OpenStreetMap and prints its copyright address, as ODbL asks for printed works. | Easy |
| 3 | Sun times (sunrise, sunset, golden hour) offline: which code? | (a) **SunCalc** (BSD-2, about 10 KB, no dependencies; CLAUDE.md already names it, but it isn't installed). (b) Our own: NOAA's solar-position formulas, about 100 lines, tested against NOAA's tables. | **(a) SunCalc**, since CLAUDE.md lists it; (b) if Kevin would rather add no dependency. Either way: worked out on the computer from the location's place on the map (3.4) and the day's date, offline; golden hour while the sun is between 6° above and 4° below the horizon, blue hour from 4° to 6° below; shown in the location's time zone (decision 7). | Easy |
| 4 | A map on the printed call sheet? | (a) **No image**: the address, coordinates and the full map link printed, plus a QR code of the link (QR needs a small encoder: a dependency, or about 300 lines of our own). (b) **A map image** made from tiles fetched through Rust, once the map question (docs/HANDOFF.md, "Map: before building") is answered: which tiles, and whether their policy allows printing them. | **(a) now, (b) when the map is decided.** A crew member scans the QR to get directions on their phone, which is what the map is for on set. QR: a dependency (`qrcode-generator`, MIT, no dependencies) if Kevin agrees, else our own. | Easy |
| 5 | What does a call sheet keep, and what does it read live? (CLAUDE.md: views never copy data.) | (a) Everything live: the call sheet is a view of the day. (b) Live while drafting; **issuing** saves the PDF as sent and marks the call sheet issued (version, time), and from then on it shows "Changed since issued" when what it reads changes. | **(b).** What was sent to the crew is a record of its own (a PDF kept on the day, as 3.11's attachments are), and the live one says when it no longer matches. Issuing also locks the day's scenes' shot numbers in the same step (Phase 4 notes, Kevin Nov 3), so one undo takes both back. Re-issuing is version 2, 3…; nothing is sent from the app (publishing is Phase 9). | Medium: issued PDFs are kept in files people received |
| 6 | Whose times does it hold? | (a) One crew call for the day (`call_time`, 3.7). (b) Crew call, plus a call per department and per person where it differs; cast with a call time, HMU (hair, makeup) ready and on-set times. | **(b)**, kept on the call sheet record as overrides of the day's crew call (nothing set means the crew call), so a change to the day moves everyone who follows it. The HMU check (CLAUDE.md) reads these. | Easy |
| 7 | Which time zone? | (a) This computer's. (b) The location's, from its place on the map. | **(b)** on the call sheet, said as the zone's name ("EST"): a crew in New York preparing a shoot in Lima reads Lima's sunrise. Working out a zone from coordinates offline needs a time-zone boundary table (about 100 KB) or a lookup: a question to settle in 4.3; until a location has a place, the computer's zone. | Medium |
| 8 | Paper size | (a) Letter. (b) A4. (c) By this computer's region, with a choice in Export. | **(c)**, as the week's first day is (3.17): Letter in the US, Canada, Mexico and others that use it; A4 elsewhere. | Easy |
| 9 | Who is on a day's call sheet? | (a) Everyone in Cast and crew (Phase 3's default). (b) Those on the day: a person is on it once added, from the day's crew list, which 3.8's Confirmed already keeps per person. | **(b)**, with "Add everyone" for a small crew; a cast member is on it when a scene that day has a character they play (3.13's ids). Contact details print only for the people on that call sheet (CLAUDE.md). | Easy |

Waiting for Kevin: all of them. Decisions 1 (a CLAUDE.md network rule), 3 and 4 (dependencies) can't be decided by
